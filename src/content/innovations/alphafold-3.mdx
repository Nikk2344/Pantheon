## From a chain to an assembly

Proteins often act with other molecules. AlphaFold 3 broadens prediction to assemblies that can include DNA, RNA, ligands, and chemical modifications. The 2024 paper evaluates several interaction categories rather than presenting one universal accuracy score for every possible biological system.

A central architectural change is its diffusion-based generation of atomic coordinates. This is related to the broad idea of learning denoising transformations, but the inputs, representation, and scientific evaluation differ from ordinary image generation.

## What the evidence supports

The authors compare predicted structures with reference structures and report gains across several interaction tasks. The result supports improved prediction on the evaluated problems. It does not establish a complete model of a cell or a substitute for measurements of dynamics, affinity, and function.

## Work through a proposed use

Suppose a researcher wants to know whether changing one residue might affect a contact with a small molecule. Predicted structures could suggest where to look and which comparison to investigate. The useful next question is what observation would distinguish the proposed explanation from an alternative.

A beautiful molecular rendering is not that observation. The contact might be misplaced, a conformation might be missing, or the actual biological conditions might differ from the prediction setup. Confidence and validation need to accompany the picture.
