## The problem it addressed

A recurrent translation model processes a sequence through successive states. Attention had already allowed a decoder to consult relevant parts of an input instead of relying only on one compressed representation. The transformer made attention the main mechanism connecting positions, dispensing with recurrence in its sequence architecture.

Its original evidence came from machine translation. That is a narrower claim than saying that the 2017 paper already contained every feature of a modern conversational model.

## Read a sentence with context

Consider: “The animal did not cross the street because it was tired.” A reader connects “it” with “animal.” Attention permits information from different positions to be combined when constructing a token's representation. This sentence is an illustration of the motivation, not a claim about a measured attention head in a particular model.

Multiple heads can form different mixtures. A subsequent feed-forward transformation processes the result. Repeating these operations builds a contextual representation, while positional information prevents the input from becoming merely an unordered collection of words.

## Why the distinction between training and use matters

Parallel processing of positions during training does not imply that every model generates all output tokens simultaneously. An autoregressive decoder typically produces a continuation one token at a time. The encoder-decoder transformer, encoder-only BERT, and decoder-based language models are related designs with different objectives and information access.

## Inspect the claim

When someone says a system “uses transformers,” ask about the training data, objective, attention mask, context length, and evaluation task. Those choices determine what the architecture is trained to do. [BERT](/innovations/bert) illustrates representation learning, while [InstructGPT](/innovations/instructgpt) illustrates additional training for instruction following.
