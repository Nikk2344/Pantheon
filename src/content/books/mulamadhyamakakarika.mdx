A Buddhist treatise in verse that analyses concepts such as causation, motion, self and nirvana, arguing that things lack intrinsic nature (svabhāva) and are empty (śūnya). It is the founding work of the Madhyamaka school.

## Why it matters

It is a central work of Mahayana Buddhist philosophy and a major influence across South, East and Central Asia.
