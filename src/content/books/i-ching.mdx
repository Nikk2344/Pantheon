A Chinese text built on 64 hexagrams of broken and unbroken lines, each with brief judgments and line statements, originally used for divination. A body of later commentaries, known as the Ten Wings, turned it into a book of cosmology and ethics.

## Why it matters

It is counted among the Five Classics of the Confucian canon and has influenced Chinese philosophy, divination and cosmology for over two thousand years. Western readers including Leibniz were interested in its structure.
