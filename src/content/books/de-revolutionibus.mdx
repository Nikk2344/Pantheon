A technical astronomical treatise that places the Sun near the centre of the planetary system and treats the earth as a planet that rotates daily and orbits annually. It develops geometric models and tables in the manner of Ptolemy’s Almagest.

## Why it matters

It launched the debate that led to the replacement of earth-centred astronomy, and the modern view of the solar system.
