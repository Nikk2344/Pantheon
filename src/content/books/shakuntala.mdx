A Sanskrit play in seven acts based on an episode in the Mahabharata, in which King Dushyanta meets and marries Shakuntala, loses his memory of her through a curse, and is reunited with her after a ring is found. It mixes verse and prose, with Sanskrit and Prakrit speech.

## Why it matters

It is regarded as the finest work of the poet Kalidasa and a classic of Sanskrit drama. William Jones’s English translation in 1789 helped introduce Sanskrit literature to Europe, and Goethe admired it.
