A collection of anecdotes drawn from conversations between Feynman and his friend Ralph Leighton, covering his childhood, his work at Los Alamos, his teaching and his curiosity about everything from safecracking to drumming. The subtitle is "Adventures of a Curious Character".

## Why it matters

It shaped the public image of Feynman and conveyed a spirit of playful scientific curiosity to many readers.
