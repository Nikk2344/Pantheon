A short Chinese treatise in 13 chapters on strategy, planning, deception, terrain and the conduct of armies. It argues that war is costly and that winning without fighting, through preparation and information, is preferable.

## Why it matters

It has been read for centuries in East Asia and, since the 18th century, in the West, and is cited well beyond the military in business, politics and sport.
