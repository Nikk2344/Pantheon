A novel in two parts about an aging gentleman who, driven mad by reading chivalric romances, sets out as a knight-errant with his companion Sancho Panza. It plays with the line between illusion and reality, and in Part 2 the characters have read Part 1.

## Why it matters

It is often called the first modern novel and has influenced fiction ever since, with its mix of comedy, irony, metafiction and humane understanding of its characters.
