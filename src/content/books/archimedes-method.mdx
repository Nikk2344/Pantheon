A letter to Eratosthenes describing how Archimedes used a mechanical balancing argument to discover results about areas and volumes, which he then proved by rigorous geometric methods. It was long lost and survives mainly in the Archimedes Palimpsest.

## Why it matters

It shows how a leading ancient mathematician found results, not just proved them, and anticipates ideas later developed in integral calculus.
