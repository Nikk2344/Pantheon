The oldest surviving Japanese chronicle, presenting myths, genealogies and legendary history from the creation of the world to the early imperial court. It was compiled at the request of Empress Genmei.

## Why it matters

It is a primary source for early Japanese myth, language and the self-presentation of the imperial house.
