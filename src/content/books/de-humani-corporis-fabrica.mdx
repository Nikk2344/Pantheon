A seven-book anatomical atlas based on human dissection, illustrated with detailed woodcuts, published at Basel. It corrects many errors inherited from Galen, whose anatomy had relied partly on animal dissection.

## Why it matters

It established observation of the human body as the basis of anatomy and set a standard for scientific illustration.
