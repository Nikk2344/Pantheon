A dialogue that criticises the Aristotelian four elements and the alchemists’ three principles as unsupported by experiment. Boyle argues for a corpuscular view in which matter consists of particles and chemistry is open to experimental inquiry.

## Why it matters

It is regarded as an important step in separating chemistry from alchemy and in framing the idea of chemical elements.
