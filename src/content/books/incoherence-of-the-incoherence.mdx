A reply to al-Ghazali, defending Aristotelian philosophy and arguing that Ghazali had misrepresented the philosophers or that his arguments failed. Averroes also distances himself from Avicenna’s views on several points.

## Why it matters

It is a key document of the medieval debate over reason and revelation, and its Latin reception influenced European scholasticism.
