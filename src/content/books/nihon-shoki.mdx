A court history of Japan in thirty volumes, written in Classical Chinese, running from the age of the gods to the late 7th century. It includes variant versions of many myths and records of contact with Korea and China.

## Why it matters

Together with the Kojiki, it is the main source for Japan’s early history and mythology.
