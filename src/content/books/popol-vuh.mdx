A K’iche’ Maya narrative of creation and the adventures of the Hero Twins, Hunahpu and Xbalanque, followed by the genealogies and history of the K’iche’ lords. It survives in a version written in the Latin alphabet in the mid-16th century.

## Why it matters

It is the most important surviving source for Mesoamerican mythology of the highlands of Guatemala, and a key text for understanding Maya thought and its endurance after the Spanish conquest.
