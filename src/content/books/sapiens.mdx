A sweeping history of Homo sapiens from the Stone Age to the present, organised around the cognitive, agricultural and scientific revolutions. Harari stresses shared fictions such as money, nations and religions as the basis of large-scale cooperation.

## Why it matters

It became an international bestseller and a common entry point to big-picture history and debates about humanity’s future.
