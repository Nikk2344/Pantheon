A collection of stories, dialogues and arguments associated with Zhuang Zhou, covering the relativity of viewpoints, freedom, death and skilled activity. It includes the "butterfly dream" and the parable of the butcher Ding.

## Why it matters

It is a central Daoist text and a major work of both Chinese philosophy and literature.
