A dialogue over four days between Salviati, Sagredo and Simplicio, comparing the Ptolemaic and Copernican systems. It argues for the Copernican view using telescopic observations, the tides argument and the principle of relative motion.

## Why it matters

It is a landmark of scientific writing in the vernacular, and its publication led to Galileo’s 1633 trial.
