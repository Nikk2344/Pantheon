A treatise on the human good, which Aristotle identifies with eudaimonia, activity of the soul in accordance with virtue. It analyses the moral and intellectual virtues, voluntary action, pleasure and friendship.

## Why it matters

It is the founding text of virtue ethics and shaped medieval, Islamic and modern moral philosophy.
