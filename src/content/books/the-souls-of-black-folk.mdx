A collection of essays on the lives and prospects of African Americans after Reconstruction, combining sociology, history, memoir and cultural criticism. Du Bois introduces the idea of "double consciousness" and criticises the accommodationism of Booker T. Washington.

## Why it matters

It is a landmark of American social thought and civil rights literature that still shapes discussion of race.
