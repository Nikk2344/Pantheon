A Sanskrit mathematical treatise in verse, forming part of Bhaskara II’s Siddhanta Shiromani. It covers arithmetic, geometry, measurement and series through worked problems.

## Why it matters

It was a standard teaching text in India for centuries and is a classic of Indian mathematics.
