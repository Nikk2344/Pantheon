A dramatic poem in two parts about the scholar Heinrich Faust, who makes a wager with the devil Mephistopheles, seeking experience beyond human limits. Part One centres on Faust and Gretchen, and Part Two ranges widely through myth, politics and nature.

## Why it matters

It is regarded as a central work of German literature and a major treatment of ambition, striving and knowledge. The Faust legend has since become shorthand for the cost of limitless desire.
