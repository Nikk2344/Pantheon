A very long novel following several aristocratic families in Russia during the Napoleonic era, including Napoleon’s 1812 invasion. It combines fictional characters with real events and essays on the philosophy of history.

## Why it matters

It is one of the best-known novels in world literature and a landmark of realism and of the historical novel. Its argument that individuals matter less than historical forces provokes debate.
