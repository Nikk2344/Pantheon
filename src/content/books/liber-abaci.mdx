A Latin treatise that teaches the Hindu-Arabic numerals and methods of calculation to merchants and scholars, with many worked commercial problems. It also includes the rabbit problem that generates what is now called the Fibonacci sequence.

## Why it matters

It helped introduce Hindu-Arabic numeral arithmetic to European commerce and mathematics.
