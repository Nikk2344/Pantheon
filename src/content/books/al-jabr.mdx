A practical treatise that gives systematic methods for solving linear and quadratic equations, together with geometric justifications and applications to inheritance and land measurement. It is written in words, not symbols.

## Why it matters

The word “algebra” comes from its title, and it presented algebra as a discipline with its own procedures.
