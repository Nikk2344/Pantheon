A Sanskrit epic of about 24,000 verses in seven books (kandas), telling of Prince Rama’s exile, the abduction of his wife Sita by Ravana, and the war to recover her. Later retellings in many languages, in regional literature and performance, have extended and reinterpreted the story.

## Why it matters

It is the model for the Sanskrit literary poem (kavya) and one of the most retold stories in South and Southeast Asia, appearing in poetry, theatre, dance and visual arts across many cultures.
