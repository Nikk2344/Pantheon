A Latin epic in 12 books about the Trojan hero Aeneas, who escapes the fall of Troy and travels to Italy, becoming an ancestor of the Romans. It was written under Augustus and echoes both the Iliad and the Odyssey.

## Why it matters

It became the national epic of Rome and a model for later European epic, and was central to Western education. Its portrayal of duty, loss and empire is often read as both celebratory and troubled.
