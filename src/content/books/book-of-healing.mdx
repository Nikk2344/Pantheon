A large philosophical encyclopedia covering logic, natural science, mathematics and metaphysics, in the Aristotelian tradition. Avicenna developed an original metaphysics there, including the argument from contingency for a necessary existent.

## Why it matters

It was a major source for medieval philosophy in Islamic and Latin traditions.
