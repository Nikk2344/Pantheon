A 700-verse dialogue in 18 chapters, set on the eve of the Kurukshetra battle in the Mahabharata, in which the warrior Arjuna hesitates and Krishna gives a long discourse on duty, action, knowledge and devotion. It brings together several strands of Indian thought, including Samkhya, yoga and devotional theism.

## Why it matters

It is among the most commented-on texts in the Hindu tradition and has been read as a guide to action, ethics and devotion. Medieval and modern thinkers interpreted it in very different ways, as the commentaries of Shankara and Ramanuja show.
