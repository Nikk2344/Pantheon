A detailed history of nuclear physics and the Manhattan Project, from the discovery of fission to the bombing of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. It covers the scientists, engineers, politicians and the moral questions their work raised.

## Why it matters

It is the standard narrative history of how the bomb was developed and a model of popular scientific history.
