Six meditations in which Descartes doubts everything that can be doubted, finds certainty in his own existence as a thinking thing, and argues for God’s existence and for a distinction between mind and body. The first edition appeared with objections from other thinkers and Descartes’s replies.

## Why it matters

It set the agenda for modern epistemology and the mind–body problem.
