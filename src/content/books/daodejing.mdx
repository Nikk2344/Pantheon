A short collection of brief, poetic chapters on the dao (the Way) and de (virtue or power), on rulership, and on living in accord with natural patterns. It praises humility, simplicity and non-coercive action (wuwei).

## Why it matters

It is a core text of Daoism and one of the most translated works in world literature.
