Using long-run tax and wealth data from many countries, Piketty examines how wealth and income inequality have evolved since the 18th century. He argues that when the return on capital exceeds economic growth, wealth tends to concentrate, and proposes a global progressive tax on capital.

## Why it matters

It put inequality at the centre of economic debate and made historical data on wealth widely discussed. The English translation appeared in 2014.
