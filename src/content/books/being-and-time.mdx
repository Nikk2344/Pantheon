Heidegger revives the question of the meaning of being by analysing the human way of existing, which he calls Dasein. The work examines being-in-the-world, anxiety, death, conscience and temporality.

## Why it matters

It reshaped phenomenology and was foundational for existentialism, hermeneutics and later continental thought.
