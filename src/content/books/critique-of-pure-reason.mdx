Kant asks what we can know independently of experience and argues that space and time, and categories such as causality, are contributions of the mind that structure experience. He concludes that we can know phenomena but not things as they are in themselves.

## Why it matters

It reoriented epistemology and metaphysics and is a reference point for nearly all later European philosophy.
