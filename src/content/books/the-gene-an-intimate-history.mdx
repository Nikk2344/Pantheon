A history of genetics from Mendel and Darwin through the discovery of DNA to modern genome sequencing and gene editing. It weaves the science together with the author’s own family history of mental illness and with the ethical history of eugenics.

## Why it matters

It gives a readable, humane account of how the idea of the gene developed and of the moral questions raised by reading and editing the genome.
