Through dialogues, puzzles and essays, Hofstadter explores self-reference and formal systems, linking Gödel’s incompleteness theorems, Escher’s drawings and Bach’s music. He uses these to ask how meaning and a self could arise from meaningless symbol manipulation.

## Why it matters

It introduced many readers to logic, computation and the philosophy of mind, and inspired a generation of students of AI and cognitive science.
