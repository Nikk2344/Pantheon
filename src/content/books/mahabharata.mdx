A very long Sanskrit epic, about 100,000 verses in 18 books (parvans), centred on the war of succession between two related families, the Pandavas and the Kauravas, at Kurukshetra. It also contains extensive digressions on law, duty, statecraft, philosophy and myth, including the Bhagavad Gita.

## Why it matters

It is one of the two great Sanskrit epics and has shaped literature, drama, art and ethical debate across South and Southeast Asia for over two thousand years. It is often cited as one of the longest poems in the world.
