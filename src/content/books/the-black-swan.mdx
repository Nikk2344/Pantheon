Taleb argues that rare, high-impact and unpredictable events shape history and markets far more than ordinary models allow. He criticises reliance on bell-curve statistics and overconfident forecasting.

## Why it matters

It made "black swan" a common phrase for rare shocks and popularised scepticism toward naive risk models, especially after the 2007-2008 financial crisis.
