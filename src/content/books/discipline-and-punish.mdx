Foucault traces the shift in European punishment from public spectacles of torture to modern prisons and disciplinary institutions. He argues that modern power works through surveillance, training and normalisation of bodies, using Bentham’s Panopticon as an image.

## Why it matters

It shaped the humanities and social sciences, influencing studies of power, institutions and surveillance.
