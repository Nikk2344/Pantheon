An oral epic of West Africa that tells of Sundiata Keita, a prince who overcame childhood disability and exile to defeat the sorcerer-king Sumanguru and found the Mali Empire in the 13th century. Griots, hereditary bards, have transmitted it for generations.

## Why it matters

It is among the best-known African epics and a key source on Mande ideas of kingship and history. It illustrates how oral tradition preserves and shapes the memory of a society.
