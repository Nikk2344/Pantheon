The oldest Chinese poetry anthology, containing 305 poems divided into folk songs (feng), court songs (ya) and hymns and sacrificial songs (song). The poems cover love, work, war, feasting, lament and ritual.

## Why it matters

It is one of the Five Classics and shaped the Chinese poetic tradition, education and moral commentary for over two millennia. It is a key window on early Zhou society and language.
