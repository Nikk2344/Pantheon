A Qing-dynasty novel about the decline of the aristocratic Jia family, centred on the love triangle between Jia Baoyu, Lin Daiyu and Xue Baochai. It has hundreds of characters and mixes realism with myth and allegory.

## Why it matters

It is generally counted among the Four Great Classical Novels of Chinese literature and is the subject of an extensive scholarly field called “Redology”.
