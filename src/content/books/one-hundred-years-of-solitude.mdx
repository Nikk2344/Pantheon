A novel following seven generations of the Buendía family in the fictional town of Macondo, blending the everyday with the fantastic. It touches on civil war, banana-company exploitation and the cycles of history.

## Why it matters

It is a landmark of Latin American literature and of magical realism, and helped bring global attention to the Latin American “Boom”. García Márquez received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1982.
