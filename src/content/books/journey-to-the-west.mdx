A Ming-dynasty novel in 100 chapters loosely based on the 7th-century pilgrimage of the monk Xuanzang to India to fetch Buddhist scriptures. In the novel he is accompanied by the Monkey King Sun Wukong, Zhu Bajie and Sha Wujing, and faces many monsters.

## Why it matters

It is one of the Four Great Classical Novels of China and a source for countless later stories, plays, films and games. It combines adventure, humor, folk religion and allegory.
