A Malayalam work that explains mathematics and astronomy of the Kerala school, giving reasoning and proofs for results found in earlier texts. It treats infinite series and trigonometric functions.

## Why it matters

It preserves rationales, uncommon in Indian texts, for results associated with Madhava on infinite series.
