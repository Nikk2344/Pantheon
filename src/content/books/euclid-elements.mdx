A systematic treatise in thirteen books that organises plane and solid geometry and number theory around definitions, postulates, common notions and numbered propositions with proofs. It collects and orders earlier Greek results, including work attributed to Eudoxus and Theaetetus.

## Why it matters

It set the pattern of deductive proof from stated assumptions, and remained a standard geometry text for roughly two thousand years.
