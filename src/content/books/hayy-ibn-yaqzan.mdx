A philosophical tale of a child raised alone on an island who comes to knowledge of nature and of God through reason and reflection. It then meets a man from a religious society and compares the two paths.

## Why it matters

It is a key work of Andalusian philosophy and was read in Latin and English translation in early modern Europe.
