A 30-volume medical encyclopaedia from Islamic Spain, whose final volume on surgery describes operations, cautery and illustrated surgical instruments. It was compiled by a court physician in Córdoba.

## Why it matters

Its surgical section, translated into Latin, influenced European surgery for several centuries.
