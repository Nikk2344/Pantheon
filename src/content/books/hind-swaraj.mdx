A short book written as a dialogue between an editor and a reader, written by Gandhi on a ship returning from London to South Africa. It criticises modern civilisation and violent politics and sets out his idea of self-rule.

## Why it matters

It is the clearest early statement of Gandhi’s ideas on nonviolence, self-rule and critique of industrial modernity.
