Russell, a leading AI textbook author, argues that building machines to optimise fixed objectives is dangerous as they become more capable. He proposes designing systems that are uncertain about human preferences and learn them from human behaviour.

## Why it matters

It sets out a technical-minded approach to AI safety from within mainstream AI research, written for general readers.
