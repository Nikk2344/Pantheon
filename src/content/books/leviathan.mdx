Hobbes argues that without a common power people live in a "state of nature" of fear and conflict, and that they should authorise a sovereign to secure peace. The work also covers human nature, law and the relation of church and state.

## Why it matters

It is a foundation of social-contract theory and of modern arguments about sovereignty and political obligation.
