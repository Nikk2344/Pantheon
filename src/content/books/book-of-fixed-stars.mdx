An illustrated astronomical catalogue that revises Ptolemy’s star catalogue, giving positions, magnitudes and drawings of the constellations. It also includes Arabic star names and traditions.

## Why it matters

It is a landmark of observational astronomy and includes an early record of what we now know as the Andromeda Galaxy.
