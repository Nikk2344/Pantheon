A collection of 246 problems with solution methods, arranged in nine chapters covering fields, proportion, taxation, engineering, simultaneous linear equations and right triangles. It became the central mathematical classic of imperial China.

## Why it matters

It documents sophisticated algorithms, including an elimination method for linear systems, long before similar methods appeared in Europe.
