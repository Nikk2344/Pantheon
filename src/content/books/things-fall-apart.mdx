A novel set in the Igbo village of Umuofia in southeastern Nigeria in the late 19th century, following the warrior Okonkwo as colonial missionaries and administrators arrive. It portrays Igbo society from within, before and during European contact.

## Why it matters

It is widely taught and regarded as a founding work of modern African literature in English, offering an African answer to colonial-era European portrayals of Africa.
