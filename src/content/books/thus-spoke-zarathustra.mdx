A philosophical work in a biblical, poetic style in which a wandering prophet descends from the mountains to teach. It introduces the "overman", the will to power and the idea of eternal recurrence, and announces the "death of God".

## Why it matters

It is Nietzsche’s most famous work and a landmark of philosophical literature, influencing existentialism, literature and music.
