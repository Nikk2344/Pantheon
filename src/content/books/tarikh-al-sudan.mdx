A history of the Songhai Empire and the Niger bend written in Timbuktu in Arabic, covering the cities of Timbuktu and Djenné, the rulers of Mali and Songhai and the Moroccan conquest of 1591.

## Why it matters

It is one of the principal written sources for the history of West Africa before the 17th century, and is an African-authored chronicle.
