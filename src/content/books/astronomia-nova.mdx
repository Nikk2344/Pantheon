Kepler’s long account of his study of the orbit of Mars using Tycho Brahe’s observations. It presents the first two of his laws, that planets move in ellipses with the Sun at one focus and sweep equal areas in equal times.

## Why it matters

It replaced circular orbits with ellipses and showed how a theory could be tested against precise data.
