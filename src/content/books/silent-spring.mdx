Carson documents the harm that widespread use of synthetic pesticides, especially DDT, did to birds, wildlife and ecosystems, and questions the confidence of the chemical industry and regulators. The title evokes a spring without birdsong.

## Why it matters

It is widely credited with helping launch the modern environmental movement and with prompting public scrutiny of pesticide use in the United States.
