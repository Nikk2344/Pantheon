A text written as an address for the Jat-Pat-Todak Mandal of Lahore, which withdrew its invitation after seeing it; Ambedkar published it himself. It argues that caste is a social order upheld by religious sanction, and that it must be destroyed rather than reformed.

## Why it matters

It is a foundational work of anti-caste thought and of modern Indian political thought.
