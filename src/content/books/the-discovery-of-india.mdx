A reflective history of India from ancient times to the colonial period, written during his imprisonment at Ahmednagar Fort (1942–1945). It combines history, philosophy and personal reflection.

## Why it matters

It is an influential statement of Indian nationalism and of Nehru’s view of a plural Indian civilisation.
