A collection of short verses, 423 in the Pali version, in 26 chapters, on ethical conduct, the mind, and the path to the end of suffering in Buddhist teaching. It belongs to the Khuddaka Nikaya in the Pali Canon and has parallel versions in other early Buddhist languages.

## Why it matters

It is among the most widely read and translated Buddhist texts, and often serves as a first introduction to early Buddhist ethics and practice.
