The introduction to Ibn Khaldun’s universal history, in which he analyses the causes of the rise and decline of dynasties, the roles of group solidarity (asabiyya), economy, urban and nomadic life, and knowledge. It aims to establish a science of human social organisation (ʿumrān).

## Why it matters

It is regarded as a pioneering work of historiography and social theory, and is widely cited in sociology and economics.
