A collection of notes by a Song dynasty official and polymath on astronomy, mathematics, geology, magnetism, medicine, technology and the arts. It records observations and practical details of his time.

## Why it matters

It is a major source for the history of science and technology in medieval China, including early descriptions of the magnetic needle compass.
