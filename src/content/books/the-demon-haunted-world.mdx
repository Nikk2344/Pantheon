Sagan defends scientific thinking against pseudoscience, superstition and credulity, discussing claims such as UFO abductions and witch hunts. He offers a set of tools for critical thinking that he calls a "baloney detection kit".

## Why it matters

It remains a widely cited introduction to scientific scepticism and to the social value of scientific literacy.
