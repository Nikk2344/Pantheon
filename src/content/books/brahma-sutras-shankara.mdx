The Brahma Sutras are terse aphorisms that systematise the teaching of the Upanishads on Brahman. Shankara’s commentary reads them as teaching non-dualism (Advaita), that Brahman alone is ultimately real and the individual self is not different from it.

## Why it matters

It is a basic text of Vedanta and the principal source for classical Advaita Vedanta, which later Vedanta schools such as Ramanuja’s answered.
