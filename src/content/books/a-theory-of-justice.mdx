Rawls proposes "justice as fairness": principles of justice are those that free and equal persons would choose in an imagined "original position" behind a "veil of ignorance". He argues for equal basic liberties and for inequalities that benefit the least advantaged.

## Why it matters

It revived systematic political philosophy and became the reference point for later debates on distributive justice.
