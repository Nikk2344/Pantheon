Wiener proposes cybernetics as a general study of communication and control in animals, machines and societies, centred on feedback and information. The book mixes mathematics with discussion of computers, the nervous system and social implications.

## Why it matters

It helped found a cross-disciplinary field that influenced control theory, computing, cognitive science and early AI.
