A dialogue in which Socrates and companions ask what justice is and whether the just life is better than the unjust one. The inquiry runs through an imagined ideal city, a three-part account of the soul, and the images of the Sun, the Divided Line and the Cave.

## Why it matters

It set agendas for Western political philosophy, ethics, metaphysics and education that later thinkers either developed or argued against.
