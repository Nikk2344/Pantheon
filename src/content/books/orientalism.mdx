Said argues that Western scholarship, literature and art produced a style of thought that portrayed "the Orient", especially the Middle East, as exotic, backward and different, in ways tied to European imperial power. He draws on Foucault’s idea of discourse and on literary analysis.

## Why it matters

It was a founding text of postcolonial studies and changed how scholars think about representation and power.
