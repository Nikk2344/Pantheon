An epic in 24 books following Odysseus’s ten-year journey home from Troy to Ithaca, and the household and son he finds struggling in his absence. Its structure moves between his wanderings, told in part by himself, and events on Ithaca.

## Why it matters

It shaped the genres of the adventure story, the homecoming tale and the unreliable narrator. Themes of hospitality, identity and cunning recur throughout later literature.
