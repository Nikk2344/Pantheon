Spinoza presents metaphysics, psychology and ethics in the form of definitions, axioms and propositions. He argues that God or Nature is the single substance of which all things are modes, and that human freedom lies in understanding the causes of our emotions.

## Why it matters

It is a landmark of rationalism and an influential source for later debates on determinism, emotion and religion.
