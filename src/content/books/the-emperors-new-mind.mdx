Penrose surveys computability, Gödel’s theorem, relativity and quantum mechanics, and argues that human consciousness involves processes that cannot be captured by any algorithm. He suggests that a new physics may be needed to understand the mind.

## Why it matters

It is a prominent critique of strong claims for artificial intelligence and sparked long debate about mind, mathematics and physics.
