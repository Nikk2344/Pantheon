A short Sanskrit work in verse on mathematics and astronomy, covering arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, the motion of planets and eclipses. It is divided into four sections.

## Why it matters

It is among the oldest surviving Indian mathematical astronomy texts by a named author and shaped later Indian work.
