A Chinese universal history in 130 chapters covering from legendary rulers down to the reign of Emperor Wu of Han. It combines annals, chronological tables, treatises on subjects such as ritual and economics, hereditary houses and a large set of biographies.

## Why it matters

It set the model for China’s dynastic histories and made biography a central historical form. It also treats ordinary people, merchants and rebels alongside rulers.
