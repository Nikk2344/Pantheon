A polemical history of the economic exploitation of Latin America from colonial extraction of silver and sugar to 20th-century foreign investment. It argues that the continent’s poverty is tied to the wealth of other regions.

## Why it matters

It was hugely influential in Latin American debates about dependency and was banned by several military governments.
