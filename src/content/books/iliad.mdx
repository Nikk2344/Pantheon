An epic in 24 books set in the tenth year of the Trojan War, centred on the anger of Achilles after a quarrel with Agamemnon and its consequences, ending with the burial of Hector. It was composed in dactylic hexameter within an oral poetic tradition.

## Why it matters

Along with the Odyssey it became the base text of Greek education and a model for later Western epic. It treats war, honour, grief and mortality with unusual even-handedness toward both sides.
