Beauvoir examines the situation of women through biology, history, psychoanalysis, myth and lived experience, arguing that womanhood is largely a social and cultural construct and that women have been treated as the "Other". The work is in two volumes.

## Why it matters

It is a foundational text of modern feminist thought and of existentialist ethics applied to social life.
