A comprehensive two-volume account of electric and magnetic phenomena in terms of fields, building on Faraday’s ideas. It develops the equations of the electromagnetic field and the electromagnetic theory of light.

## Why it matters

It laid the basis of classical electromagnetism and led to later work on radio waves and relativity.
