Hegel traces the development of consciousness through successive forms, each exposing its own limits, toward absolute knowing. The book includes the discussions of self-consciousness, the master–slave relation and the history of culture.

## Why it matters

It influenced Marxism, existentialism, critical theory and much of 19th–20th century thought.
