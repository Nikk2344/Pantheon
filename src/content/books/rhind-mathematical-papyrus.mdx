An Egyptian mathematical text copied by the scribe Ahmes, containing tables and 84 problems on arithmetic, fractions, areas and volumes. It says it is copied from an older document.

## Why it matters

It is among the best sources for ancient Egyptian mathematics and its methods for fractions.
