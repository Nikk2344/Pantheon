Companion to the 1980 television series, the book surveys the history of astronomy and scientific inquiry from antiquity to the space age. Sagan covers the origin of the universe, stars, planets and the possibility of life elsewhere.

## Why it matters

It inspired many readers to pursue science and set a model for popular science writing and broadcasting.
