A Persian epic of roughly 50,000 couplets recounting the mythical and historical past of Iran from the first kings to the Arab conquest in the 7th century. It includes the stories of Rostam and Sohrab, Zahhak and many other figures.

## Why it matters

It is regarded as the national epic of the Persian-speaking world and is credited with helping preserve the Persian language and pre-Islamic memory.
