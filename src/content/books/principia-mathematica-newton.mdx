Newton’s treatise states the three laws of motion and the law of universal gravitation, and uses geometric reasoning to derive Kepler’s laws and explain tides and the motion of comets. It unified terrestrial and celestial mechanics.

## Why it matters

It became the foundation of classical mechanics and a model for mathematical physics.
