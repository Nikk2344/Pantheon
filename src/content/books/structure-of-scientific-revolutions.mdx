Kuhn argues that science alternates between periods of normal science within a paradigm and revolutionary shifts, when accumulated anomalies lead to a new paradigm. He emphasises the role of communities and judgement.

## Why it matters

It popularised the term paradigm shift and transformed debates in the history and philosophy of science.
