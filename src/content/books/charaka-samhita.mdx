A foundational Sanskrit text of Ayurveda focused on internal medicine, diagnosis, drugs, the body and ethics for physicians. It has traditional sections on principles, diagnosis, body, senses, treatment and success.

## Why it matters

It is one of the two classical sources of Ayurvedic medicine and a major source for early Indian ideas of health and disease.
