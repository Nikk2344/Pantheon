A Sanskrit treatise on statecraft, administration, law, economics, taxation, espionage and foreign policy, organised in 15 books. It describes in great detail how a king should run a kingdom and deal with rivals.

## Why it matters

It is the most important surviving ancient Indian work on political economy and statecraft, offering a systematic and pragmatic view of government.
