Kahneman summarises decades of research with Amos Tversky and others on judgement and decision making. He describes a fast, intuitive mode of thought and a slower, deliberate one, and shows how heuristics lead to systematic biases.

## Why it matters

It is a central popular account of behavioural economics and cognitive psychology, and influenced policy, business and public discussion of rationality.
