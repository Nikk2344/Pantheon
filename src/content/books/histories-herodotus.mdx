A prose work, later divided into nine books, on the origins and course of the Greco-Persian Wars, with long digressions on the geography, customs and history of Egypt, Persia, Scythia, Libya and other peoples. It mixes eyewitness reports, hearsay and stories, often noting when he doubts his sources.

## Why it matters

Cicero called Herodotus the “father of history”, and the work is a main source for Greek and Persian history. It is also valued for its early ethnographic curiosity about other cultures.
