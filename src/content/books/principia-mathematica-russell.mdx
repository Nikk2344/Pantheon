A three-volume work that attempts to derive mathematics from logical axioms using a formal symbolic system and a theory of types to avoid paradoxes. Its famous proof that 1 + 1 = 2 appears long after the start.

## Why it matters

It was a landmark of logicism and of modern symbolic logic and helped inspire Gödel’s incompleteness theorems.
