Dawkins presents evolution from the viewpoint of genes as the units that are copied and selected, with organisms as the vehicles that carry them. The book discusses cooperation, altruism and conflict in this light, and introduces the term "meme" for a unit of cultural imitation.

## Why it matters

It popularised the gene-centred view of evolution and shaped public and academic debate about altruism, selection and culture.
