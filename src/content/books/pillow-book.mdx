A collection of observations, lists, anecdotes and opinions by a lady-in-waiting at the Heian imperial court, notable for its sharp wit and attention to taste and detail. It is a leading example of the zuihitsu (“following the brush”) genre.

## Why it matters

It offers a lively view of court life in Heian Japan and helped establish the essay-like zuihitsu form in Japanese literature.
