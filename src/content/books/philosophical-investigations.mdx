A series of remarks that criticise a picture of language as naming objects and instead examine meaning through use in activities he calls language-games. It also discusses rule-following, private language and mental concepts.

## Why it matters

It is among the most discussed works of 20th-century philosophy and redirected attention to everyday language use.
