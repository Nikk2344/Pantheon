A set of private notes written by the Roman emperor, mostly in Greek, reflecting on duty, mortality, the cosmos and self-discipline. It applies Stoic ideas to the practical demands of a ruler’s life.

## Why it matters

It is one of the most read sources of Stoic practice and a rare first-person record of a philosopher in power.
