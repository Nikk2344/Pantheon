A late Ming illustrated encyclopaedia of agriculture and craft technology, covering grain, textiles, salt, ceramics, metals, paper, ships and more. It describes how things were produced in Chinese workshops.

## Why it matters

It is one of the best records of pre-industrial Chinese technology.
