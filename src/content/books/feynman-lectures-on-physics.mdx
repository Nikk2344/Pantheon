A three-volume set based on Feynman’s introductory physics course at Caltech in 1961–1963. It covers mechanics, radiation, heat, electromagnetism, matter and quantum mechanics with an emphasis on understanding.

## Why it matters

It is among the most widely read physics textbooks, admired for its conceptual clarity.
