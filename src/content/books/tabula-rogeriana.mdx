A geographical work and world map made for King Roger II of Sicily, describing the known world in climatic zones with text on the regions, peoples and routes. It draws on earlier geographers and travellers’ reports.

## Why it matters

It is one of the most important medieval geographies and shows exchange of knowledge between Islamic and Norman Sicily.
