A systematic medical encyclopaedia in five books covering general principles, simple drugs, diseases by body part, diseases not confined to one organ, and compound remedies. It organises Greek, Islamic and Indian medical knowledge into one ordered scheme.

## Why it matters

It served as a standard medical reference in the Islamic world and, in Latin translation, in European universities for several centuries.
