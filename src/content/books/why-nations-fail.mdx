The authors argue that national prosperity depends mainly on political and economic institutions. Inclusive institutions that spread power and opportunity foster growth, while extractive institutions that concentrate benefits in a few hands hold it back.

## Why it matters

It was a leading statement of the institutional explanation of long-run economic development and influenced policy debate.
