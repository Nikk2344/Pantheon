An ancient Egyptian medical text that mainly describes the examination and treatment of injuries, particularly of the head, neck and chest. Each case is arranged as title, examination, diagnosis and treatment.

## Why it matters

It is the oldest known surgical treatise and shows a rational, case-based approach to trauma in ancient Egypt.
