A long narrative in 54 chapters following Hikaru Genji, the son of an emperor, and later his descendants in the Heian court. It is known for psychological depth, and for a poetic style in which characters exchange waka poems.

## Why it matters

It is often described as one of the earliest novels in the world and is the central classic of Japanese literature, shaping later art and writing.
