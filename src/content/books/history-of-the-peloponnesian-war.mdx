An account, in eight books, of the war between Athens and Sparta and their allies from 431 BCE, left unfinished in the narrative of 411 BCE. It includes speeches, such as Pericles’ funeral oration, and analyses of power, fear, plague and civil strife.

## Why it matters

It is a foundation of political and military history and of the study of international relations, valued for its attention to causes, human nature and the effects of war on politics.
