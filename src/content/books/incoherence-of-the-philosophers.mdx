A critique of Avicennian philosophers on twenty points, arguing that their arguments on the eternity of the world, God’s knowledge and bodily resurrection fail on their own terms. Al-Ghazali charges them with unbelief on three of the points.

## Why it matters

It is a landmark of Islamic theology and philosophy, and the discussion of causation became a reference point for later debate.
