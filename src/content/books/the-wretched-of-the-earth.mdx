Written during the Algerian War of Independence, the book analyses colonialism, decolonisation, violence, national consciousness and the psychological effects of colonial rule. It carries a preface by Jean-Paul Sartre.

## Why it matters

It became a major text for anticolonial movements and for postcolonial thought.
