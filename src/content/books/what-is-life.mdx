Based on lectures at Dublin, the book asks how physics and chemistry can account for the events within a living cell. It proposes that the hereditary material is an aperiodic crystal carrying a code.

## Why it matters

It influenced a generation of physicists and biologists, including some who contributed to molecular biology.
