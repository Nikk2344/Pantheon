Conversations of Mencius with rulers and students, arguing that human nature has incipient moral sprouts that can be cultivated into virtue. He also argues for benevolent government and the people’s welfare.

## Why it matters

It shaped the Confucian tradition, notably the view that human nature is good, and later became a canonical text.
