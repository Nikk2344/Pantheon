A collection of tales framed by Scheherazade, who tells stories each night to postpone her execution. The stories draw on Indian, Persian, Arabic and later sources and include voyages, fables and romances; different manuscripts contain different tales.

## Why it matters

It is among the most influential works of world storytelling and has shaped Western images of the Middle East, as well as fantasy and framed narrative.
