A study in three parts of antisemitism, imperialism and totalitarian rule, seeking the elements that crystallised in Nazi Germany and Stalin’s Soviet Union. Arendt examines terror, ideology and the rise of stateless and rightless people.

## Why it matters

It is a foundational work on totalitarianism and statelessness and remains widely discussed in political theory.
