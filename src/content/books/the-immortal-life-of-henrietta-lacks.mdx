Skloot tells the story of Henrietta Lacks, a Black American woman whose cancer cells, taken in 1951 at Johns Hopkins Hospital without her knowledge, became the long-lived HeLa cell line widely used in research. The book follows both the science and her family’s later struggle to learn what had happened.

## Why it matters

It brought issues of consent, race, and ownership of human tissue in medical research to a general audience.
