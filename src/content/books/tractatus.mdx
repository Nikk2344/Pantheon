A short, numbered work claiming that language pictures facts, that the limits of language are the limits of the world, and that what cannot be said must be passed over in silence. It was written largely during the First World War and published in 1921.

## Why it matters

It strongly influenced logical positivism and 20th-century analytic philosophy of language.
