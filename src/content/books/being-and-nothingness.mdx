A phenomenological ontology distinguishing being-in-itself from being-for-itself, the conscious, free human existence. It develops the themes of freedom, bad faith, and relations with others.

## Why it matters

It is the main philosophical statement of Sartrean existentialism and shaped postwar French thought.
