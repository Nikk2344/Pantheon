Greene introduces relativity and quantum mechanics and then explains string theory, in which fundamental particles are tiny vibrating strings in extra dimensions. The book presents it as a candidate for unifying gravity with quantum physics.

## Why it matters

It was a leading popular account of string theory and brought it to a wide public audience.
