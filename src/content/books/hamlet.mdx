A tragedy about Prince Hamlet of Denmark, who is told by his father’s ghost that the new king, Claudius, murdered him, and who struggles to act. It contains the soliloquies, the play within the play and a final duel.

## Why it matters

It is one of the most performed and discussed plays in the language and a touchstone for ideas about doubt, thought and action, and the inner self.
