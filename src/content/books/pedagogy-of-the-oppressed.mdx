Written while Freire was in exile in Chile, the book argues that education should be a practice of dialogue and liberation rather than the passive depositing of knowledge. It links literacy and critical awareness to the struggle of oppressed people.

## Why it matters

It is one of the most cited works in education and shaped critical pedagogy and adult literacy movements worldwide.
