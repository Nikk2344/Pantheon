A multi-volume work that tries to present nature as an interconnected whole, from the stars to the Earth and living things. It draws on Humboldt’s travels in Latin America and on data from many fields.

## Why it matters

It shaped 19th-century ideas of nature’s unity, and influenced scientists and writers including Darwin and Thoreau.
