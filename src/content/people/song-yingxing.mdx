Song Yingxing was a late-Ming scholar who held minor official posts. He wrote Tiangong Kaiwu (1637), an illustrated account of agriculture and manufacturing techniques.

## The central question

How should the crafts that feed and clothe a society be written about?

His death date is uncertain, and the book was long better known in Japan than in China. Its accounts reflect what he observed or was told, not systematic measurement.
