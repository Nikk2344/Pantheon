Kiyoshi Oka was a Japanese mathematician. He solved major problems in the theory of functions of several complex variables, largely working alone.

## The central question

When a problem resists the standard methods, what does it take to make it yield?

His papers are difficult, and later authors reformulated them in the language of sheaves. Read him alongside later accounts.
