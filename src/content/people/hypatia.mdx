Hypatia was a philosopher and mathematical teacher in Alexandria. The surviving evidence for her writings and achievements is limited.

## The central question

What does it take to preserve and teach difficult knowledge?

Avoid attributing a convenient list of inventions to her without evidence. Her murder in 415 belongs to a complex political and religious context, not a simple slogan about science and religion.
