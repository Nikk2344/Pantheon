Hilbert was a leader of mathematics in Göttingen. He worked in algebra, geometry, and analysis, and set the direction of the foundations of mathematics.

## The central question

Can mathematics be shown to be consistent and complete?

His priority in deriving the field equations of general relativity, in November 1915, involved a brief priority dispute with Einstein. Historians still discuss who derived what, and when.
