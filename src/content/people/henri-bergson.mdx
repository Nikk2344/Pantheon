Henri Bergson was a French philosopher who distinguished lived duration from the time of science. He received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1927.

## The central question

Is lived time the same as the time on a clock?

In 1922 he debated Einstein about time, and many physicists and philosophers judged that he had misunderstood relativity. His reputation fell for decades and was revived by readers such as Deleuze.
