Mary Anning collected fossils from the cliffs at Lyme Regis, in a family of fossil sellers. Her finds helped scientists understand extinct marine reptiles.

## The central question

Who gets credit for the discovery of the past?

As a woman of modest means she was not admitted to the Geological Society, and her work was often published under the names of others. The widespread claim that the tongue twister “She sells sea shells” is about her is unproven.
