Peter Doherty is an Australian immunologist born in Brisbane. He shared the 1996 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Rolf Zinkernagel for how the immune system recognises virus-infected cells.

## The central question

How do immune cells know which infected cells to kill?

He is a living scientist, and the discovery is a joint one. Later work has refined the molecular picture of T-cell recognition.
