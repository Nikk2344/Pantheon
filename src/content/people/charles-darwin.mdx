The Beagle voyage supplied observations, specimens, and questions. Darwin’s account developed over years of subsequent reading, correspondence, and comparison.

## A population changes

Selection acts on existing variation. A trait useful in one environment may be harmful elsewhere; evolution has no universal ladder toward perfection. Reproductive success matters, not strength alone.

## An explanation with unfinished pieces

Darwin lacked the later genetic account of inheritance. His mechanism was important without resolving every question about the transmission of variation. Understanding those limits leaves room for the science that followed.
