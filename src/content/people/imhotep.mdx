Imhotep was an official under the Third Dynasty king Djoser and is traditionally linked with the design of the Step Pyramid at Saqqara. Centuries later he was remembered as a sage and healer and came to be worshipped.

## The central question

How do we separate a historical official from the legend that grows around him?

His medical fame comes from sources written long after his life, and no surviving medical text can be securely assigned to him. Treat the architect and the later deity as separate layers of the record.
