Mahavira is revered in Jain traditions as the twenty-fourth tirthankara. Chronologies and biographical traditions differ.

## The central question

What follows if avoiding harm becomes a central discipline of life?

Later Jain philosophical systems should not all be assigned directly to Mahavira. Distinguish a founding teacher, transmitted traditions, and their later intellectual development.
