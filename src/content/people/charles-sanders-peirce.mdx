Peirce was an American scientist, logician, and philosopher. He is regarded as the founder of pragmatism and a major figure in semiotics.

## The central question

How can a community of inquirers fix its beliefs?

Most of his work was unpublished at his death, and his writings are best read with a scholarly edition. His views changed over time, and the term “abduction” was used with different senses.
