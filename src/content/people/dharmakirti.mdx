Dharmakirti was a Buddhist philosopher who worked on logic and the theory of knowledge. His Pramanavarttika was a major text for later Indian and Tibetan scholars.

## The central question

What makes a perception or an inference a reliable guide?

His dates are approximate and his texts survive in Sanskrit and Tibetan, with interpretation shaped by later commentary. Different traditions read him differently.
