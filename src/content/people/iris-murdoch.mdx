Iris Murdoch was an Irish-born British philosopher and novelist. She argued that moral life is about attention to others, not only about choices.

## The central question

How does paying attention make us better or worse?

She is better known to many readers as a novelist, but the philosophy deserves independent reading. Her views of the Good draw on Plato and are not shared by most moral philosophers.
