Max Theiler, born in Pretoria, worked at the Rockefeller Foundation on viruses. He received the 1951 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for developing a vaccine against yellow fever.

## The central question

How can a weakened virus protect us from the real one?

The vaccine was developed with colleagues, notably Hugh Smith, and the Nobel citation names only Theiler. Vaccine history is team history.
