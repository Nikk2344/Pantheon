Ambedkar was an anti-caste thinker, jurist, and political leader. His Annihilation of Caste began as an address that its inviting organization refused to host.

## Equality beyond the ballot

Institutions can proclaim equality while everyday relations preserve inherited hierarchy. Ask which social arrangements make equal citizenship effective.

## Read the argument directly

The critique examines caste’s defenses as well as its consequences. Its challenge reaches education, social association, authority, and the meaning of democratic community.
