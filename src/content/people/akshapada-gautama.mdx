Gautama, also called Akshapada, is the traditional author of the Nyaya Sutra. The text lays the foundation of the Nyaya school, which examines the sources of knowledge, inference and debate.

## The central question

What counts as a good reason to believe something?

The author’s identity and the date of the text are uncertain, and the Sutra reached its present form over time. The later Navya-Nyaya school developed the system far beyond the original.
