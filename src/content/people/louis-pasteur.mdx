Pasteur began as a chemist and became a leading figure in microbiology. He worked on fermentation, on spontaneous generation, and on vaccines.

## The central question

Where do microbes come from, and what can they do to us and for us?

Historians have shown from his notebooks that he sometimes did not describe his methods fully, and his rivalries with other scientists were sharp. The germ theory was the work of many people, including Koch, Lister, and others.
