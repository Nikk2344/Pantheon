Francisco José de Caldas was born in Popayán in the Viceroyalty of New Granada. He worked on botany, astronomy and geography, and was executed in 1816 during the Spanish reconquest.

## The central question

How does a colony produce its own science?

He is sometimes called a precursor of ecology or biogeography; that reading is retrospective. Check details of his dates and posts against specialised biographies.
