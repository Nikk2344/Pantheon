Panini was a grammarian of ancient India. His Ashtadhyayi gives a rule-based description of Sanskrit in about four thousand terse rules, arranged with an explicit system of ordering and abbreviation.

## The central question

Can a language be described by a compact set of precise rules?

His dates and life are uncertain, and scholars place him between roughly the 6th and 4th centuries BCE. Treat comparisons to modern computer science as analogies, not as claims about his intentions.
