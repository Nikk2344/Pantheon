Montesquieu was a French jurist and political thinker. He wrote the Persian Letters and The Spirit of the Laws.

## The central question

How can power be arranged so that it does not become tyranny?

His views on the effect of climate on societies are now regarded as unsound, and he did not originate the tripartite division in a form that matches modern constitutions exactly.
