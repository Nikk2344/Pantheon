Heraclitus wrote in compressed aphorisms that later readers interpret through themes of change, conflict, logos, and hidden harmony.

## The central question

How can the world have order if everything is changing?

The famous river saying survives in later reports and variations. Read the fragments carefully before turning Heraclitus into a slogan that nothing at all persists.
