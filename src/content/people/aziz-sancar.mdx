Aziz Sancar is a Turkish-born biochemist who works at the University of North Carolina. He shared the 2015 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for mapping how cells repair DNA damage, with Tomas Lindahl and Paul Modrich.

## The central question

How do cells repair their own genetic information, and what does it cost when this fails?

This entry describes a living scientist. The Nobel Prize was shared, and the prize recognises three separate repair mechanisms studied by three researchers.
