Jyesthadeva was a member of the Kerala school. His Malayalam work Yuktibhasha, written c. 1530, sets out reasons for results in mathematics and astronomy.

## The central question

Who first gave a proof, and who first stated a result?

The dates are approximate. Historians credit the series to Madhava, but the surviving proofs are in later works like this one. There is no evidence that this work reached Europe in time to influence calculus.
