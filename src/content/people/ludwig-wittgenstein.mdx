Wittgenstein’s early and later works pursue philosophical problems through strikingly different methods. The later Investigations appeared after his death.

## Look for the practice

A word may request, promise, measure, joke, or describe. Ask what people are doing before treating every sentence as a picture of a fact.

## Follow a rule

What makes a new move count as following the same rule? The question connects meaning to training, shared practices, and criteria of correctness.
