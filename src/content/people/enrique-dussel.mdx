Enrique Dussel was an Argentine-born philosopher who lived mostly in Mexico and was a leading voice in the philosophy of liberation. He wrote on ethics, politics and the history of colonialism.

## The central question

Whose point of view does philosophy take for granted?

His philosophy is contested within and outside Latin America.
