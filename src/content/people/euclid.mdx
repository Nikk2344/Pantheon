Euclid’s Elements organized a vast body of mathematical knowledge into a deductive presentation. Little is securely known about his life.

## The central question

What can be established from a small set of stated assumptions?

The Elements incorporates earlier work; it is not a claim that one person invented its entire contents. Notice how a proof depends on both stated premises and a diagram’s interpretation.
