Feynman worked on quantum electrodynamics and many other areas of physics. His diagrams organize calculations of interactions; they are not photographs of particle trajectories.

## From describing to simulating

A classical description of a general quantum state grows rapidly as more systems are added. Feynman asked how a machine governed by quantum rules might reproduce quantum behavior.

## Try a tiny example

Open the [quantum computing guide](/topics/quantum-computing#playground) and compare a pair of independent qubits with an entangled pair. Our small classical simulator illustrates the rules, not a quantum speed advantage.
