Saadia Gaon was born in Egypt and became head of the academy of Sura in Babylonia. He wrote on Jewish law, biblical interpretation and philosophy, often in Judeo-Arabic.

## The central question

How can a revealed tradition be defended by reason without being reduced to it?

His philosophy draws on the Islamic kalam tradition, so its debts should be read alongside its aims. Dates and details of his life are well attested but some episodes are known from partisan accounts.
