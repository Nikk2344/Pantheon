Al-Zahrawi was a physician in Córdoba, associated with the court of the Umayyad caliphate. He is best known for the Kitab al-Tasrif, a medical encyclopedia whose surgical section describes and illustrates instruments.

## The central question

How do surgical skills get passed on in writing, and how much can pictures of instruments teach?

Details of his life are thin, and many attributions of individual surgical techniques to him are repeated without strong evidence. Treat claims that he invented a given procedure with caution.
