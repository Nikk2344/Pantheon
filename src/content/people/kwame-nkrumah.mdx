Kwame Nkrumah led the Gold Coast to independence as Ghana and served as its first prime minister and president. He also wrote on philosophy, including Consciencism, a work on ideology and decolonisation.

## The central question

Can political independence be complete without economic and intellectual independence?

He was both a thinker and a head of state, and his rule has been judged in sharply different ways. Read his philosophy separately from the assessment of his government.
