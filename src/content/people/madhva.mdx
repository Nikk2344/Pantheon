Madhva was a Vedanta philosopher of Karnataka who founded the Dvaita, or dualist, school. He held that God, individual souls and matter are really and eternally distinct.

## The central question

Can a philosophy of real difference between God and souls be argued from the same texts as non-dualism?

Dates differ among sources, and the traditional dates are used here with hedging. The school’s doctrine is described here as its own tradition presents it.
