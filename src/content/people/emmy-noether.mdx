Noether transformed abstract algebra and established a foundational connection between symmetries and conservation.

## The central question

Why do symmetries bring conservation laws with them?

Time-translation symmetry and energy conservation provide a useful example. The theorem’s assumptions matter; a vague resemblance or a discrete symmetry is not automatically the same case.
