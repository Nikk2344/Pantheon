Sridhara was an Indian mathematician known for works on arithmetic and algebra, among them the Patiganita and the Trisatika. His date and birthplace are disputed, and a best estimate puts his work around 900 CE.

## The central question

What does a practical arithmetic text reveal about everyday mathematics?

Estimates of his dates have ranged from the 7th to the 11th century, and his authorship of some works is debated. Do not rely on any single birth date.
