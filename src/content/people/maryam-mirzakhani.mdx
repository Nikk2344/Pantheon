Maryam Mirzakhani worked on the geometry and dynamics of Riemann surfaces and their moduli spaces. In 2014 she became the first woman to win the Fields Medal.

## The central question

What is the geometry of all possible geometries of a surface?

The mathematics is highly technical. Her work is best approached through her aims, and the Fields Medal citation, before the details.
