Wonhyo was a Silla Buddhist monk and scholar who wrote extensively on Buddhist texts. He is known for trying to reconcile different Buddhist schools.

## The central question

Can rival doctrines be understood as parts of a larger whole?

Many works attributed to him survive only in part, and biographical anecdotes are later and may be legendary. Rely on the extant texts.
