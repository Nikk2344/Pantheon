Rabindranath Tagore was a Bengali poet, composer and thinker, and in 1913 the first non-European to win the Nobel Prize in Literature. He also wrote on education, nationalism and humanism.

## The central question

What should education and nationalism serve, if not the narrow self?

He is better known as a poet than as a philosopher, and his views on nationalism were criticised by contemporaries, among them Gandhi, who answered him in public exchanges.
