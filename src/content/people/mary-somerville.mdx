Mary Somerville taught herself mathematics and became one of the leading science writers of the nineteenth century. Her books explained recent mathematics and physics to a wide audience.

## The central question

What does it take to see the sciences as parts of one picture?

Her work was mostly synthesis and exposition. This was a skilled and influential contribution, and it should not be mistaken for a claim of major new theorems.
