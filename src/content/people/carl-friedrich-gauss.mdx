Gauss made fundamental contributions to number theory, algebra, geometry, statistics, astronomy, and magnetism, and was careful about what he published.

## The central question

What is the deep structure beneath numbers, shapes, and measurements?

Legendre published the least-squares method first, in 1805, and Gauss claimed earlier use. Stories of his childhood, such as summing the numbers from 1 to 100, are anecdotes of uncertain detail.
