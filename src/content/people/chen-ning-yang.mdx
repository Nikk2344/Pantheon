Chen Ning Yang was a Chinese-born theoretical physicist. With Tsung-Dao Lee he proposed that parity is not conserved in weak interactions, and they shared the 1957 Nobel Prize in Physics.

## The central question

What does it mean for the laws of nature to distinguish left from right?

The experimental confirmation was by Chien-Shiung Wu and colleagues, and the Nobel did not recognise her. Weigh the theory and the experiment as two parts of one result.
