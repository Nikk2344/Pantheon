Percy Julian was an American chemist who synthesised medically useful compounds from plant sources. He is known for work on physostigmine and for making steroid hormones from soybean sterols.

## The central question

How can a plant become the raw material for medicine?

He was a leading chemist but also an industrial researcher, and some credit belongs to colleagues. Numbers of patents and similar details are best checked against the primary biographies.
