The Mūlamadhyamakakārikā investigates causation, motion, self, and time. Arguments often expose difficulties within a position rather than supply another substantial foundation.

## Dependence is not meaninglessness

A cart depends on parts and arrangement; a practice depends on conditions. The question is whether functioning requires an independent essence.
