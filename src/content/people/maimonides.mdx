Maimonides was a Jewish philosopher, legal scholar, and physician whose work engages Aristotelian philosophy.

## The central question

How should a reader respond when scripture and philosophical reasoning seem to conflict?

A denial of bodily attributes is not yet a positive description of divine nature. Ask whether removing misleading descriptions produces knowledge or merely a boundary around it.
