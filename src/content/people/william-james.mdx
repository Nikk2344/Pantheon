William James was an American philosopher and psychologist. He was a leader of pragmatism and a founder of American psychology.

## The central question

What is the point of a belief if it makes no difference to how we live?

His pragmatic view of truth was widely criticised, including by Russell and Moore, and he said he had been misunderstood. It is not the same as holding that a belief is true because it is useful.
