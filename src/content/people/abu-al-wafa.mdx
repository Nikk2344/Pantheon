Abu al-Wafa was a mathematician and astronomer who was born in Buzhgan and worked in Baghdad. He wrote on trigonometry, geometry and practical arithmetic.

## The central question

How do the tools of trigonometry get simpler and more systematic over time?

Some claims about his astronomical observations rest on later reports. Read priority claims for trigonometric functions with care, since several scholars worked on them in the same period.
