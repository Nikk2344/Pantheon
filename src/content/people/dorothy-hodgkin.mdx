Dorothy Hodgkin used X-ray crystallography to find the structures of complex biological molecules. She received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1964.

## The central question

How can we see the structure of a molecule we cannot see?

Her work depended on long collaborations and on computing, and she trained many later crystallographers. Credit in large groups should be read with attention to who did what.
