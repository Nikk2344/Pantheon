Varahamihira was an Indian astronomer and encyclopaedist of the 6th century CE. He is best known for the Pancha-siddhantika, a summary of five astronomical schools, and for the Brihat-samhita, a wide-ranging compendium.

## The central question

How did early Indian scholars compile and compare rival astronomical systems?

Much of what he wrote mixes observational astronomy with divination, and the two should be read in their own context. Details of his life rest on later tradition and on MacTutor-style estimates from his own texts.
