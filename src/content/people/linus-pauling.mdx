Linus Pauling applied quantum mechanics to chemical bonding and later to the structure of proteins. He won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 1954 and the Peace Prize in 1962.

## The central question

How can the shapes of molecules explain what they do?

He proposed an incorrect triple-helix model of DNA in 1953. His later claims about high-dose vitamin C have not been supported by clinical trials.
