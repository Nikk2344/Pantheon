Paulin Hountondji was a Beninese philosopher. He criticised the idea that African philosophy is a collective folk worldview and argued that it should be a rigorous, individual and critical discipline.

## The central question

Is African philosophy a shared worldview or an argued discipline?

The debate with the ethnophilosophers continues, and he later qualified some of his positions. Readers should follow the exchange and not take one side as settled.
