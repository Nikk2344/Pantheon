Erasmus was a Dutch humanist and Catholic priest who edited classical and Christian texts. He was among the most widely read authors of his age.

## The central question

How should a person of learning respond to a divided Christendom?

The year of his birth is uncertain, and he is given as 1466, 1467, or 1469 in different sources. He was critical of church abuses but remained within the Catholic Church, which led to attacks from both sides.
