Dalton developed an influential chemical atomic theory connecting composition with the relative weights of atoms.

## The central question

Could fixed combining ratios reveal the particles of matter?

Dalton’s atoms should not be identified with an unchanged modern theory: isotopes and subatomic structure revise key assumptions. A useful model can be both transformative and incomplete.
