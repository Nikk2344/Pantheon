Parmenides developed a radical challenge to ordinary assumptions about change, plurality, and coming-to-be through a philosophical poem.

## The central question

What can reason say when appearance and being seem to conflict?

Do not reduce Parmenides to the denial of motion without following the argument. The interest is how language, thought, and being are made to constrain one another.
