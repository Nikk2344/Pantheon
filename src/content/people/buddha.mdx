The Buddha’s teachings survive in traditions transmitted and compiled after his lifetime. Philosophical interpretation must distinguish those sources from a recoverable modern biography.

## Conditions, not isolation

Experiences arise through conditions. Investigating craving and attachment shifts attention from a fixed essence toward processes that may be changed.

## A path to investigate

Ethics, meditation, and understanding reinforce one another. Buddhist traditions disagree about many details; a short introduction cannot stand in for their diversity.
