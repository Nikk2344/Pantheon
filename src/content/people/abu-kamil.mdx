Abu Kamil was an Egyptian mathematician who extended the algebra of al-Khwarizmi. His Book on Algebra applies algebra to geometry and to indeterminate problems.

## The central question

What happens to algebra when its unknowns may be irrational?

Few details of his life are known, and his dates are approximate. His influence on Fibonacci is generally accepted but how direct it was should be checked in specialist studies.
