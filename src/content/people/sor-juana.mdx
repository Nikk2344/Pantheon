Sor Juana was a nun and writer in New Spain, born near Mexico City. She wrote poetry, plays and prose and defended women’s right to study.

## The central question

Who has the right to pursue knowledge?

Her birth year is given as 1648 or 1651 in different sources. Her late renunciation of study is debated: some see coercion, others religious conviction. The SEP entry is a survey of Latin American philosophy.
