Marcus Aurelius was a Roman emperor and a practitioner of Stoic philosophy. The notebooks known as Meditations were written for his own use.

## Read a working notebook

Repetition is part of their purpose: reminders address judgments that remain difficult in practice. They should not be treated as a sequence of formal proofs.

## Power complicates the example

An emperor’s reflections on humanity sit within an empire sustained by warfare and slavery. Admiring an ethical exercise need not turn its author into a flawless model.
