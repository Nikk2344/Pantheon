Jiddu Krishnamurti was an Indian-born speaker and writer who, in 1929, dissolved an organisation set up to support him. He taught that truth cannot be reached through any organisation, authority or method.

## The central question

Can truth be reached through any method, teacher or organisation?

He avoided systematic argument, and most of his texts are edited transcripts of talks. Read them as one person’s approach, not as academic philosophy.
