Mario Molina was a Mexican chemist who, with F. Sherwood Rowland, proposed that chlorofluorocarbons deplete stratospheric ozone. He shared the 1995 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Rowland and Paul Crutzen.

## The central question

How can a tiny trace gas threaten a planetary shield?

The 1974 argument was a prediction, confirmed by later measurements and by the Antarctic ozone hole. Policy outcomes also depended on many other people.
