Al-Biruni investigated astronomy, geography, chronology, and the intellectual traditions of India.

## The central question

How much can careful geometry tell us about a world too large to measure directly?

Claims about extraordinary numerical precision need careful attention to units, observational errors, and later reconstructions. A sound method and a perfectly accurate measurement are different achievements.
