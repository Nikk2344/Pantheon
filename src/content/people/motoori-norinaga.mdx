Motoori Norinaga was an Edo-period scholar of the kokugaku, or “national learning”, movement. He studied Japan’s ancient texts and wrote on poetry, language and the sense of things.

## The central question

Can a culture be understood by reading its oldest texts without imposing foreign categories?

His scholarship was later used in nationalist arguments, and this has coloured how it is read. Distinguish his philological method from later political uses.
