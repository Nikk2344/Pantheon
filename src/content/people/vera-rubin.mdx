Vera Rubin’s measurements of galaxy rotation curves supplied major evidence for dark matter and changed modern cosmology.

## The central question

What if galaxies rotate as though most of their mass cannot be seen?

Dark matter is not simply ordinary darkness or empty space. It is inferred from gravitational behavior, and its particle nature remains an open research question.
