Mencius developed a Confucian account of human moral potential and humane government.

## The central question

What capacities for goodness are present before deliberate moral training?

The claim that human nature is good does not say that everyone already acts well. Test how the account explains neglect, hostile conditions, and deliberate wrongdoing.
