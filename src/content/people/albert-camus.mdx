Camus explored the absurd, revolt, and solidarity through essays and literature. He resisted easy identification with existentialism.

## The central question

How should we live when the world does not answer our demand for meaning?

The absurd is not simply the claim that nothing matters. Ask how refusing false consolation can coexist with care for others.
