Nasir al-Din al-Tusi was a polymath from Tus who worked under the Ilkhanid rulers and directed the observatory at Maragha. He wrote on astronomy, mathematics, logic, ethics and theology.

## The central question

Can a better geometric model replace an inherited one without discarding the framework it came from?

A common claim is that the Tusi couple directly influenced Copernicus. Similar constructions appear in his work, but a direct line of transmission is debated, and the evidence is circumstantial.
