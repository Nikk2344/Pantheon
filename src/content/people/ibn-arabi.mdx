Ibn Arabi was an Andalusi Sufi thinker who travelled in North Africa, the Hejaz and Syria. His works, especially the Meccan Revelations and the Bezels of Wisdom, were highly influential in later Islamic thought.

## The central question

How can the divine and the world be described without collapsing one into the other?

The phrase “unity of being” is closely tied to later followers and critics, and whether it fairly summarises his own view is debated. Different traditions of reading disagree about his meaning.
