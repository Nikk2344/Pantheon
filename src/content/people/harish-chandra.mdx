Harish-Chandra was an Indian-born mathematician who spent most of his career in the United States. His work on the representations of semisimple Lie groups shaped modern harmonic analysis.

## The central question

Can symmetry be analysed even when the group is infinite and continuous?

His work is technical, and a short summary hides how long it took to develop. He trained first as a physicist under Homi Bhabha and Paul Dirac before turning to mathematics.
