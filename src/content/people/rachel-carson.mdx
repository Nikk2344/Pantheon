Rachel Carson was a marine biologist and writer. Her books on the sea and then on pesticides reached a very wide public.

## The central question

What are the long-term effects of what we put into the environment?

The chemical industry attacked the book, and some critics later blamed it for limits on DDT use against malaria. Its actual argument was against indiscriminate spraying, and the later history of DDT policy is more complicated than either version.
