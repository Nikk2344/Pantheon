Kwasi Wiredu was a Ghanaian philosopher who taught in Ghana and the United States. He argued for examining African thought critically and for conceptual decolonisation.

## The central question

What is lost when we think in borrowed concepts?

His account of Akan concepts is an interpretation and has been contested by other African philosophers. The consensual-democracy proposal is a normative argument, not a description of how Akan states always ran.
