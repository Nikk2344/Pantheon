Wangari Maathai was a Kenyan biologist and professor of veterinary anatomy. She founded the Green Belt Movement and won the 2004 Nobel Peace Prize.

## The central question

Can planting trees also be a way to build civic life?

She is better known as an activist than as a research scientist, and the movement’s effects on forest cover are harder to quantify than its social effects. Cite figures for tree counts with care.
