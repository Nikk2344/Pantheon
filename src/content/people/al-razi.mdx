Al-Razi was a physician and thinker born in Rayy who worked in Rayy and Baghdad. He wrote a large medical compendium and a short clinical work distinguishing smallpox from measles.

## The central question

How should a physician weigh inherited authority against his own clinical observation?

Dates are approximate and sources differ by a few years. His philosophical and religious views survive largely through quotations by critics, so they should be read with that filter in mind.
