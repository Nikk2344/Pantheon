Hume studied understanding as part of human nature. He asked which beliefs survive scrutiny and why we continue to expect and act.

## Sequence is not necessity

Observing one event follow another creates an expectation through repetition. But necessity is not directly displayed to the senses. What licenses the move beyond observation?

## Ordinary life remains

Hume did not expect people to stop anticipating the future. Habit helps explain why we do so. A belief’s psychological origin and a non-circular philosophical proof are different things.
