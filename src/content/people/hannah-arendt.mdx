Arendt was a Jewish thinker who fled Nazi Germany and later lived in the United States. She investigated totalitarianism, political action, and judgment.

## Plurality matters

A public world contains different perspectives. Action opens possibilities that cannot be fully controlled like the manufacture of an object.

## A contested account

Her interpretation of Eichmann remains disputed. The phrase associated with it should never be read as making his crimes ordinary or excusable.
