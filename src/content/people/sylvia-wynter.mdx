Sylvia Wynter was a Jamaican scholar and writer, born in Cuba, whose essays examine how Western ideas of the human have been constructed. She taught at Stanford and wrote on literature, history and science.

## The central question

Who is included in the definition of the human?

Her prose is dense and her synthesis of science and humanities is contested. Read it alongside secondary introductions, and check dates for her late works.
