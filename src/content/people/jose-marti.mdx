José Martí was a Cuban writer and organiser of the independence movement against Spain. He died in battle in 1895 at Dos Ríos.

## The central question

What would independence mean if it only replaced one master with another?

Martí’s legacy is claimed by many political camps, in Cuba and abroad, so check the claims against his own texts.
