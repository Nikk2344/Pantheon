Working in the medieval Islamic world, Ibn al-Haytham treated optical questions as problems requiring mathematics and physical investigation together.

## The eye is not the whole story

Vision involves the path of light, the eye, and the interpretation of what is received. His work explored more than the slogan that light enters the eye.

## Knowledge travels

The Book of Optics circulated in Latin and influenced European optical traditions. A history focused only on modern Europe misses accumulated work behind later instruments. His biography is less certain than the survival and influence of his writings.
