Solomon ibn Gabirol was a Jewish poet and philosopher from Málaga who worked in Zaragoza. His Arabic philosophical work was known to Latin readers as Fons Vitae, under the name Avicebron.

## The central question

Can all created things share a single underlying matter, even spiritual ones?

For a long time his Latin readers did not know he was Jewish. His dates are uncertain, and his death is often placed between the 1050s and 1070.
