Gödel was a logician whose theorems of 1929–1931 changed how mathematics and logic understand proof, truth, and formal systems.

## The central question

What can a formal system prove about itself?

The theorems apply to specific formal systems. They do not show that “truth is relative” or that human knowledge is limited in general.
