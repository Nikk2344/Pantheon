Venkatraman Ramakrishnan, born in Chidambaram, India, is a structural biologist who shared the 2009 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for studies of the structure and function of the ribosome.

## The central question

How does the ribosome read a message accurately enough to build proteins?

The 2009 prize was shared with Thomas Steitz and Ada Yonath, whose groups also solved ribosome structures. He is living, so the record is still being added to.
