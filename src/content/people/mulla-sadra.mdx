Mulla Sadra was a philosopher from Shiraz who worked mainly in Safavid Iran. He combined Avicennan, Illuminationist, Sufi and theological ideas into a new system.

## The central question

Is existence more basic than essence, and can being itself change?

Dates are given differently in the sources. His reception has been shaped by later schools, so modern descriptions can differ about what his system is.
