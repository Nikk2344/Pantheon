Bartolomé de las Casas was a Spanish Dominican friar born in Seville who spent years in the Americas. He argued that Indigenous peoples had rights and that Spanish treatment of them was unjust.

## The central question

What do conquerors owe the people they conquer?

His numbers and rhetoric were later used by Spain’s rivals, and some historians question his figures. Early in his career he supported African slavery, which he later rejected. Take care to read him in context.
