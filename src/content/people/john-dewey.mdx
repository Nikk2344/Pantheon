John Dewey was an American philosopher and educator. He tied philosophy to experience, inquiry, and the practice of democratic life.

## The central question

What should an education for democracy look like?

His name is linked to “progressive education”, but his own views were often critical of how those ideas were put into practice. His writing is demanding and repays slower reading.
