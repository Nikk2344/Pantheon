Tycho Brahe was a Danish nobleman who built an observatory at Hven and made the most accurate naked-eye measurements of his time. His records gave Kepler the data for his laws.

## The central question

What changes when observation becomes far more precise than theory?

The story of his death, from a burst bladder, is repeated widely, but modern analyses of his remains have been used to question some of the older claims about it.
