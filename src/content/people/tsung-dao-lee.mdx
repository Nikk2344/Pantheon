Tsung-Dao Lee was a Chinese-born theoretical physicist. With Chen Ning Yang he proposed the non-conservation of parity in weak interactions and shared the 1957 Nobel Prize in Physics.

## The central question

How is a long-held symmetry tested, and what changes when it fails?

He and Yang later parted ways, and accounts of the split come mainly from those involved. Treat them as partial testimony.
