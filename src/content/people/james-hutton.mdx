James Hutton was a Scottish physician and farmer who argued that the Earth’s rocks are shaped by slow, repeated cycles of erosion, deposition, and uplift.

## The central question

What can slow processes we can observe today tell us about the age of the Earth?

His writing was hard to read, and his ideas became widely known largely through John Playfair’s 1802 summary and Charles Lyell. He did not use the word “uniformitarianism”.
