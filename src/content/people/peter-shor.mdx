Shor’s algorithm connects a number-theoretic problem to quantum period finding. It requires a suitable quantum computer and careful classical processing of measurement results.

## Precision about speed

No polynomial-time classical factoring algorithm is known, but that is not a proof that one cannot exist. The algorithm also does not make every difficult problem easy.

The [quantum computing guide](/topics/quantum-computing) introduces gates, phase, and measurement. Its two-qubit playground does not implement Shor’s factoring algorithm.
