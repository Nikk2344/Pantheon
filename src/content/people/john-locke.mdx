Locke’s writings on knowledge, toleration, and government shaped later liberal thought. Their influence coexists with serious exclusions and his entanglement with colonial institutions.

## Experience has limits

Asking where an idea comes from is not the same as proving every belief formed from experience. His account distinguishes degrees of knowledge and judgment.

## Who is included?

Read arguments for liberty alongside questions about property, slavery, and the people excluded from political equality.
