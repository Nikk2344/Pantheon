Marx combined philosophy, political economy, journalism, and revolutionary politics, often working with Friedrich Engels. His writings changed over time and invite competing interpretations.

## Relations behind things

A commodity can appear as a thing with an independent value. Marx asks which relations among people are expressed through that appearance.

## Separate the questions

Assessing his analysis, his political proposals, and regimes that later invoked his name requires related but distinct arguments.
