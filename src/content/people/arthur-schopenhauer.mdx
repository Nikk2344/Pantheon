Schopenhauer developed a pessimistic philosophy influenced by Kant and his reading of Indian traditions. Those encounters also reflect the limitations of the translations available to him.

## Wanting again

Obtaining an object may end one desire without ending the cycle of wanting. Is lasting satisfaction possible on those terms?

## A demanding comparison

His use of Asian texts should not be mistaken for a neutral account of those traditions. Read the original traditions on their own terms as well.
