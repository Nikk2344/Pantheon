Raman pursued experimental physics in India and helped build research institutions. He investigated acoustics as well as optics.

## A faint signal

Most scattering preserves the incident frequency. The weaker shifted component reveals energy exchanged with molecules. Detecting it required separating that signal from stronger surrounding light.

## A discovery becomes a tool

Raman spectroscopy turns frequency differences into evidence about materials. The useful information lies in the spectrum’s structure, not simply a visible colour change.
