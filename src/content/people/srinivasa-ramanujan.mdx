Ramanujan produced deep formulas in number theory, infinite series, partitions, and modular forms, often recorded with little conventional proof.

## The central question

How can astonishing patterns become mathematics others can prove, extend, and test?

Admiring intuition should not mean ignoring proof. Ramanujan’s notebooks are powerful partly because later mathematicians could verify, refine, and extend many claims.
