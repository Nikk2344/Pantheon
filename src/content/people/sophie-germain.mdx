Sophie Germain studied mathematics largely by herself. She wrote to Lagrange and to Gauss under a male pseudonym, and she won a prize from the Paris Academy for her work on elastic surfaces.

## The central question

What can be accomplished when institutions will not let you in?

Her earlier entries for the prize contained errors, and her theory of plates was later corrected and extended. She is a good example of work done outside the formal channels, not outside rigour.
