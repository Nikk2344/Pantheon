Zera Yacob is the author, or traditionally the attributed author, of the Hatata (Inquiry), a 17th-century Ethiopian text. It describes a life of reasoning in a cave and criticises rival claims of revelation.

## The central question

Can reason alone judge between rival religions?

The authorship and date of the Hatata are debated: some scholars see it as largely the work of Zera Yacob, others suspect a later European hand. Present it as a text with a contested attribution.
