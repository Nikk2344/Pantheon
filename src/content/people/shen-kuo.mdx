Shen Kuo was a Song-dynasty official and scholar. His Dream Pool Essays, c. 1088, collect observations on astronomy, geology, technology and much else.

## The central question

What does a polymath notice when he records the world as it is, not as authority says it is?

Many famous “firsts” attributed to him depend on how early comparable observations elsewhere were recorded. Read the essays as a rich source, not as proof of priority.
