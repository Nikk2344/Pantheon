Euler was one of the most prolific mathematicians in history. He worked in analysis, number theory, graph theory, mechanics, and astronomy, and shaped much modern notation.

## The central question

What patterns appear when one problem is solved in a way that works for many?

Euler worked with infinite series in ways that were not rigorous by later standards. Many of his results were put on firmer foundations afterwards.
