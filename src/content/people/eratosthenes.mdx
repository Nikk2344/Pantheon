Eratosthenes was born in Cyrene and worked in Alexandria, where he was associated with the Library. He is best known for an estimate of the Earth’s circumference and for a method of listing prime numbers.

## The central question

How far can you measure a planet from a stick in the ground?

His own writings are lost and are known through later authors, so the exact method and the length of the unit (the stadion) behind his famous figure remain debated. Attribution of the sieve to him rests on later reports.
