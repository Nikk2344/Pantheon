Suhrawardi was a philosopher who founded the school of Illumination. He wrote in Arabic and Persian and was executed in Aleppo.

## The central question

Is some knowledge gained by direct acquaintance rather than by definition and argument?

The reasons for his execution are told in different ways and mix political and religious factors, so they should not be reduced to a single cause. The school’s “ancient Persian wisdom” is itself a claim made within his texts.
