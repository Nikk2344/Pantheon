Fanon wrote as a psychiatrist, anti-colonial thinker, and political participant, examining racism, violence, liberation, and the psychic life of colonial rule.

## The central question

What does colonial domination do to the body, language, and imagination?

Fanon’s work should not be flattened into a simple endorsement of violence. Read the argument in its colonial context and alongside his psychiatric and humanist concerns.
