Swami Vivekananda was a monk and teacher who presented Vedanta to audiences in India and the West. He addressed the 1893 Parliament of the World’s Religions in Chicago and founded the Ramakrishna Mission in 1897.

## The central question

Can an ancient school of thought speak to the modern world?

His writings are collected in later editions that vary, and his interpretation of Vedanta was shaped by his own time and aims. Historians debate how far his Vedanta was a modern reinvention.
