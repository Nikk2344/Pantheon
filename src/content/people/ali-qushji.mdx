Ali Qushji was an astronomer from Samarkand who worked with Ulugh Beg and later settled in Ottoman Istanbul. He wrote on astronomy, mathematics and theology.

## The central question

How does scientific knowledge move when scholars move from one court to another?

The reading of his remarks on the Earth’s motion is debated: he did not adopt a moving Earth. Dates of birth and death vary slightly in sources.
