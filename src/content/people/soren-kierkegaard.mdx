Kierkegaard used pseudonyms, irony, and indirect communication to explore existence and Christian faith. A pseudonymous speaker should not automatically be equated with the author.

## The reader is implicated

A text may force its reader to confront a decision rather than deliver a doctrine. Ask who is speaking and what kind of life that voice represents.

## Beyond a catchphrase

His treatment of faith is more complex than an endorsement of any unsupported belief.
