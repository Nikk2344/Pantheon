Jennifer Doudna is an American biochemist who studies RNA and the CRISPR-Cas9 system. She shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Emmanuelle Charpentier.

## The central question

Who should decide how powerful gene-editing tools are used?

Many groups contributed to CRISPR science, and the field’s history includes a prominent patent dispute. A debate on human germline editing, including the 2018 case of edited babies in China, continues.
