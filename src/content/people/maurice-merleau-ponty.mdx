Merleau-Ponty was a French phenomenologist. He argued that the lived body is central to perception and understanding.

## The central question

What if perception begins in the body and not in a detached mind?

The last book was left unfinished, and the published text includes working notes, so it should be read with care. His break with Sartre over politics in the 1950s is part of the story.
