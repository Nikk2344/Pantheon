Sun Simiao was a Tang-dynasty physician traditionally honoured as a leading medical figure. His dates are uncertain, and some accounts give him an improbably long life.

## The central question

What does a physician owe to every patient, regardless of rank or wealth?

The life dates reflect tradition, and the exact text of the surviving works reflects later editing. Separate the historical physician from his later reputation as a “king of medicine”.
