Wollstonecraft challenged accounts that trained women for dependence while blaming them for its effects. Her arguments belong within wider debates about revolution, education, and rights.

## Change the conditions

If a group is denied serious education, what can its resulting opportunities establish about natural ability? The explanation must include institutions.

## Continue the question

Later feminist thinkers extend and contest this approach. Equality of formal rights alone may leave substantial social inequalities intact.
