William of Ockham was an English Franciscan and a leading medieval logician and philosopher. He argued for a nominalist approach to universals.

## The central question

Should we multiply entities when fewer will do?

“Ockham’s razor” is usually quoted as “entities are not to be multiplied beyond necessity”, but this precise form is not found in his works, and he used the principle in a theological context.
