The Banu Musa were three brothers, Muhammad, Ahmad and al-Hasan, active in Baghdad in the ninth century. They were scholars and patrons connected to the translation movement and wrote on geometry, astronomy and mechanics.

## The central question

What happens when mathematics and mechanical devices are developed side by side?

Dates are approximate, and it is often unclear which brother did what. Whether every device was actually built, rather than described, is not certain.
