Katalin Karikó is a Hungarian-born biochemist. With Drew Weissman she showed how to modify messenger RNA so that it could be used in vaccines. They shared the 2023 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

## The central question

What does it take to keep working on an idea that funders do not yet support?

The vaccines also rely on work by many others, including on lipid nanoparticle delivery. Accounts that treat the prize as a single-handed discovery are too simple.
