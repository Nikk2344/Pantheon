Paulo Freire was a Brazilian educator who worked on adult literacy and critical pedagogy. His best-known book is Pedagogy of the Oppressed.

## The central question

Is teaching a way of handing over knowledge or of building it together?

The book was written in 1968 and appeared in Spanish and English in 1970; it was not published in Brazil until 1974. His ideas are applied and debated widely, and critics question how they work in practice.
