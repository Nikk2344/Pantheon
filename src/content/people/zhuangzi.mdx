Zhuangzi names both a thinker and a composite book. The Inner Chapters are traditionally associated with him; the wider collection includes other voices.

## The butterfly question

The dream story unsettles an easy confidence about the boundary between dreamer and dreamed. It works as an invitation to inquiry, not a scientific demonstration.

## Why stories?

Humor, exaggeration, and impossible conversations interrupt habits of judgment. Treating every character as a direct spokesman can flatten the text.
