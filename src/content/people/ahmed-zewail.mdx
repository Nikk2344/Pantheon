Ahmed Zewail was an Egyptian-born chemist who worked at Caltech. He received the 1999 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for studying chemical reactions on the femtosecond time scale.

## The central question

What becomes visible when you can watch a chemical reaction as it happens?

The term “father of femtochemistry” is a common shorthand, but the field built on lasers and techniques developed by many researchers.
