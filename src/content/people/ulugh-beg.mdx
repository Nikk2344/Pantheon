Ulugh Beg was a Timurid ruler of Samarkand and an astronomer. He built a large observatory in Samarkand and sponsored a new catalogue of stars and tables.

## The central question

What does it take to run a research observatory as an institution rather than as one person’s project?

The tables are the work of a team, and how much of each part is Ulugh Beg’s own is hard to separate. He was killed in 1449 in a power struggle, and the circumstances are told in different ways.
