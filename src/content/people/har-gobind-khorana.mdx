Har Gobind Khorana was an Indian-born biochemist who shared the 1968 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. He synthesised nucleic acids of defined sequence and used them to help work out how the genetic code is read.

## The central question

How can chemical synthesis be used to read the genetic code?

The Nobel Prize was shared with Robert Holley and Marshall Nirenberg, and the genetic code was cracked by several groups. Treat his role as one major part of a collective effort.
