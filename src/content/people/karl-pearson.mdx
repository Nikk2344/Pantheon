Karl Pearson was an English mathematician who helped found mathematical statistics. He worked at University College London and co-founded the journal Biometrika.

## The central question

When is a pattern in data more than chance?

He was a strong advocate of eugenics and made scientific claims about race that are now rejected. He also had a long and bitter dispute with Fisher.
