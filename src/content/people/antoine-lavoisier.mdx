Lavoisier helped transform chemistry through quantitative experiments, oxygen-based explanations, and chemical nomenclature.

## The central question

What changes when every material entering and leaving an experiment is weighed?

The work depended on collaborators, including Marie-Anne Paulze Lavoisier, and on research by others who investigated gases. Scientific revolutions are not solitary performances.
