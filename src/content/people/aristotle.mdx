Aristotle studied at Plato’s Academy but developed distinctive methods. He gathered examples, classified, and asked what kind of explanation each subject needs.

## Different kinds of explanation

His four explanatory categories concern matter, form, the source of change, and purpose. They do not all correspond to the narrower sense of cause used in modern mechanics.

## A legacy to examine critically

His biology and politics contain claims we should reject, including defences of slavery and hierarchy. Lasting influence does not make every conclusion correct. The argument below isolates the distinction between valid inference and true starting points.
