Archimedes joined rigorous geometry with mathematical investigations of equilibrium and floating bodies.

## The central question

How can geometry reveal the balance of forces?

The famous bath story is a later anecdote. The mathematical work supplies firmer evidence than the dramatic scene commonly used to introduce it.
