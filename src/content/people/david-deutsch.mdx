Deutsch’s work connects fundamental physics with the theory of computation. His 1985 paper gave a foundational account of a universal quantum computer.

## An algorithm needs structure

A superposition is not a list of answers that can all be read out. Gates must arrange interference so that measurement reveals useful information.

## Start with interference

In the [circuit playground](/topics/quantum-computing#playground), compare H–H with H–Z–H. A phase change that initially leaves probabilities unchanged can change the final result.
