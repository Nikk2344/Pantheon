Galileo made instruments part of an argument. A telescope could show something unexpected, but the observation still needed repetition and interpretation.

## Slowing down a difficult problem

Free fall happens quickly. Inclined planes made accelerated motion easier to measure and helped Galileo connect distance with elapsed time. A mathematical relationship became answerable to an experiment.

## What the evidence establishes

The phases of Venus and Jupiter’s moons challenged established astronomy in different ways. His conflict with church authorities should not flatten those arguments into one dramatic moment. His later work on motion helped prepare the ground for Newton.
