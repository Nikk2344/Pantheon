Epicurus founded the Garden, a philosophical community that connected an atomist account of nature with an ethics of pleasure.

## Enough can be a discovery

A desire may be natural and necessary, natural but unnecessary, or driven by empty opinion. Examine whether satisfying it actually reduces distress.

## A live objection

If death is not experienced, does that settle whether losing a possible future is bad? The distinction between suffering and deprivation keeps this ancient argument alive.
