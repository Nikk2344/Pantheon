Anton Wilhelm Amo was born in Axim, on the coast of present-day Ghana, and educated in Germany. He studied and taught philosophy at Halle, Wittenberg and Jena and wrote on mind and knowledge.

## The central question

How does a mind relate to a body that can feel?

Many biographical details are uncertain, including the date and circumstances of his death after returning to the Gold Coast. His early text on the legal status of Africans in Europe is lost.
