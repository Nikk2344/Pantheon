Hooke was curator of experiments at the Royal Society and a skilled instrument maker and architect. His work ranged from microscopy to elasticity and the theory of gravitation.

## The central question

What do we see when we look more closely, and how much of the order of nature can be measured?

He had a long and bitter dispute with Newton. There is no portrait of him known with certainty, and some of his work survives only partly.
