Philippa Foot was an English moral philosopher at Oxford. She helped revive virtue ethics and developed a naturalist account of goodness.

## The central question

Does moral judgement have the same kind of grounding as other judgements about living things?

The trolley case was given its name by Judith Jarvis Thomson, and her own use of it was a small part of the 1967 paper. Her naturalism is disputed.
