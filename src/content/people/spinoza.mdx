Spinoza developed a systematic philosophy presented through definitions, axioms, and demonstrations. The geometrical format makes dependencies visible without making its premises beyond dispute.

## An embodied mind

Thought and extension are not two independent substances in his account. This sharply revises the Cartesian picture.

## A question about freedom

If an action has causes, when is it more truly ours? Compare acting through adequate understanding with being driven by forces we barely grasp.
