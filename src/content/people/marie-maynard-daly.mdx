Marie Maynard Daly was an American biochemist. She is widely described as the first Black woman to earn a PhD in chemistry in the United States (Columbia, 1947).

## The central question

How do diet and cell chemistry together shape heart disease?

Claims about being first depend on how the record is defined, so the wording here is hedged. Her research was mostly done in collaboration, as is usual in biochemistry.
