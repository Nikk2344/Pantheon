Turing made foundational contributions to computation, wartime cryptanalysis, and mathematical biology.

## The central question

What can a precisely specified computing procedure do—and what can it never decide?

An impossibility result about algorithms is not a claim that every particular instance is unknowable. Distinguish a universal solving procedure from methods that work in restricted cases.
