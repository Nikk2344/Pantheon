Zu Chongzhi was a mathematician and astronomer of the Southern dynasties. He is credited with a very precise value for π and a new calendar.

## The central question

How accurately can a circle’s ratio be pinned down with only arithmetic?

His own book on the method is lost and its content is known mainly through later citation. Details of how he obtained the bounds are therefore inferred.
