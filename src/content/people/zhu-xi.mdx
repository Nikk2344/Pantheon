Zhu Xi was a Song-dynasty scholar who synthesised earlier Neo-Confucian thought. His commentaries on the Four Books shaped education in East Asia for centuries.

## The central question

What does it mean to understand the world and one’s own nature as parts of one order?

The label “Neo-Confucianism” covers varied thinkers, and critics such as Wang Yangming disagreed with Zhu Xi. Read his system as one influential position.
