Berkeley challenged material substance while defending ordinary experience within an immaterialist philosophy.

## The central question

What is added when we posit matter beyond every possible experience?

Immaterialism does not mean that objects disappear whenever you turn away in Berkeley’s complete account. Examine the role of divine perception rather than replacing the theory with a caricature.
