Ibn Bajja was a philosopher and scientist from Zaragoza who worked in Almoravid Spain and North Africa. He wrote on logic, natural philosophy, the soul and politics.

## The central question

Can a person reach intellectual fulfilment alone, or only inside a good society?

Some writers have claimed a direct line from his critique to Galileo, but this is disputed, and the evidence is indirect. Many of his works survive incompletely.
