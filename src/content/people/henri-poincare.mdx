Poincaré worked across pure mathematics, celestial mechanics, and the philosophy of science. He is often called the last universalist of mathematics.

## The central question

What can be known about a system that cannot be solved exactly?

He found a serious error in the first printed version of the three-body memoir and replaced it with a corrected one. His part in special relativity is real, but the question of priority with Einstein is debated.
