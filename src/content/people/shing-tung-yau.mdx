Shing-Tung Yau is a Chinese-born American mathematician working in geometry and analysis. He won the Fields Medal in 1982.

## The central question

How can analysis reveal the global shape of a space?

His work is highly technical, and the physical relevance of Calabi–Yau spaces rests on string-theory models that are not experimentally established.
