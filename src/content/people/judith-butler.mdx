Butler’s work examines gender, recognition, vulnerability, and the norms through which subjects become intelligible.

## The central question

How do repeated social practices make identities appear natural?

Performativity is not the claim that everyone can change social position by choosing a role. Ask how repetition is constrained and where possibilities for revision arise.
