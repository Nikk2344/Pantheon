Amid political disorder, Confucius looked to learning and exemplary conduct as resources for renewal. How people can live well together remained a practical concern.

## Formation before formula

Respect is not exhausted by obeying an instruction. The Analects connects conduct to attention, judgment, and dispositions developed through practice.

## Reading a transmitted tradition

The Analects was compiled after Confucius’s life, not written as his autobiography. Later thinkers developed and contested this inheritance. Translating ren simply as kindness, or li simply as etiquette, can conceal the philosophical complexity.
