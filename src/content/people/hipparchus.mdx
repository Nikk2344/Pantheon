Hipparchus was a Greek astronomer who combined Babylonian records with Greek geometry. Most of what we know of his work comes from Ptolemy and other later writers.

## The central question

What does a good star catalogue make possible that a good story about the sky does not?

His main works are lost. Details of the catalogue and the table of chords are reconstructed from Ptolemy’s Almagest and from commentators.
