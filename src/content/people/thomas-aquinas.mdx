Aquinas was a Dominican theologian whose philosophical work engages Aristotle, Islamic thinkers, and Christian traditions. His texts often stage objections before offering a reply.

## Read the objections too

The structure of a disputed question helps identify exactly where an argument turns. The strongest objection may clarify the intended scope of the answer.

## Avoid a shortcut

The Five Ways are not the simple claim that everything has a cause. Their premises and distinctions need individual examination.
