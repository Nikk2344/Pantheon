Émilie du Châtelet was a French natural philosopher who wrote on physics and metaphysics. She translated Newton’s Principia into French, with commentary.

## The central question

What does it take to make a new physics intelligible?

She is often mentioned as Voltaire’s companion. Her own work stands apart from that role. How far she was an original author and how far an interpreter of others is debated.
