Adam Smith was a Scottish moral philosopher of the Enlightenment. He wrote The Theory of Moral Sentiments and An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations.

## The central question

How do self-interest and sympathy fit together in a society?

He is often portrayed as a defender of unregulated markets. His own text discusses justice, public works, education, and the risks of merchants’ influence, and it should be read in full.
