Judah Halevi was a Jewish poet and thinker from Spain who wrote in Hebrew and Arabic. His philosophical work, the Kuzari, is a dialogue about religion and reason.

## The central question

Is a living tradition better grounded in philosophical proof or in shared historical experience?

The conversation is a literary frame, and the historical conversion of the Khazar court is itself debated. His own voice should be read through the dialogue and not simply identified with one speaker.
