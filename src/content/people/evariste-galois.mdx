Galois died at 20 after a duel. In a few manuscripts he linked the solvability of equations to the structure of their symmetries.

## The central question

Why can some equations be solved by formulas and others not?

The story of his final night and the duel is full of legend. The mathematics survives in his papers, while the circumstances of his death are less certain.
