Born enslaved in Hierapolis, Epictetus later taught philosophy. His student Arrian preserved the Discourses and the shorter Encheiridion.

## Practice before slogans

Someone criticizes your work. You cannot command their opinion, but you can investigate the criticism and decide how to respond. Stoic practice does not require pretending that circumstances are pleasant.

## Social roles still matter

His ethics includes obligations within relationships. Distinguishing what depends on us is not a blanket excuse for indifference to injustice.
