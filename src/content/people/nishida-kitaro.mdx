Nishida Kitaro was a Japanese philosopher and founder of the Kyoto School. He combined Western philosophy with the traditions of East Asian thought, especially Zen Buddhism.

## The central question

Is there an experience prior to the split between the one who knows and what is known?

His writing is dense and his terms are hard to render in English. His wartime relation to nationalism is debated, so consult scholarly discussion.
