Wang Yangming developed a Neo-Confucian account of moral knowing, self-cultivation, and the unity of knowledge and action.

## The central question

Can someone truly know the good while never acting on it?

Consider someone who describes generosity accurately but consistently acts selfishly. Which sense of knowledge does the unity thesis call into question?
