Kabir was a poet-saint of the Bhakti period in northern India. Poems attributed to him criticise both ritual religion and sectarian division and appear in several traditions, including the Sikh scripture.

## The central question

Can truth be found outside the boundaries of organised religion?

His dates are uncertain, and most poems were transmitted orally, so their attribution to him is often disputed. The wording of any poem depends on which manuscript tradition is used.
