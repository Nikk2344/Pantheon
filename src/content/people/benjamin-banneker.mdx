Benjamin Banneker was a free Black man in Maryland who taught himself astronomy and calculation. He published almanacs and assisted in the 1791 survey of the federal territory.

## The central question

What can self-taught observation do when formal schooling is denied?

Many popular stories, including a clock built from memory and a claim that he reconstructed the city plan, are weakly documented or legendary. His letter to Thomas Jefferson in 1791 is well attested.
