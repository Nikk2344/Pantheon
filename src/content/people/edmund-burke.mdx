Edmund Burke was an Irish-born politician and writer in Britain. He wrote on aesthetics and then on politics, including a famous critique of the French Revolution.

## The central question

How much should inherited institutions count against abstract principles?

Burke supported the American colonists and criticised abuses in India, but opposed the French Revolution. Both his supporters and critics have used him selectively.
