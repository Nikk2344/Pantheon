Galen was a physician in the Roman Empire who wrote a very large number of works on anatomy, physiology, and the logic of medicine. His system dominated medicine in Europe and the Islamic world for well over a thousand years.

## The central question

How far can animal anatomy and logic carry a theory of the human body?

Much of his anatomy comes from animals and was later corrected, notably by Vesalius and Harvey. The size of his output also means that many works survive only in Arabic or Latin versions, and some are spurious.
