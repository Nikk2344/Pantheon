Madhava of Sangamagrama is regarded as the founder of the Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics. His work is largely known from later authors.

## The central question

How can an infinite sum be used to compute a finite number?

Most of his own writings are lost, so details come through later authors such as Nilakantha and Jyesthadeva. There is no established evidence that this work reached Europe in time to influence the European calculus.
