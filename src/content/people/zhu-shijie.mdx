Zhu Shijie was a Yuan-dynasty mathematician. His Jade Mirror of the Four Unknowns (1303) extends polynomial methods to systems with up to four unknowns.

## The central question

How far can the “celestial element” method be extended to many unknowns?

Little is known of his life, and his dates are approximate. The Jade Mirror was lost in China for a time and recovered later.
