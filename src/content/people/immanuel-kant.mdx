Kant sought to explain the possibility of knowledge while limiting claims that outrun experience. His ethical work connects obligation to rational autonomy.

## More than a slogan

Universalizing a maxim requires specifying the action, circumstances, and purpose. Simply asking whether an outcome is unpleasant misses the structure of the test.

## Critique the critic

Kant’s writings include racist and sexist judgments. Reading his claims about universal dignity critically includes examining these failures.
