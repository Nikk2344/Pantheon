Cheikh Anta Diop was a Senegalese historian and scientist who argued that ancient Egyptian civilisation had African roots and that African history had been neglected. He also ran a radiocarbon dating laboratory in Dakar.

## The central question

Whose history is told, and on what evidence?

His central claims about Egypt have been influential but are debated by Egyptologists, who accept African connections while disputing parts of his racial and linguistic arguments. Present them as claims, and check them against specialist literature.
