Carlos de Sigüenza y Góngora was a scholar of New Spain, born in Mexico City, who taught mathematics and astrology at the university. He also wrote on history, geography and astronomy.

## The central question

Can comets be read as signs or as bodies moving through the sky?

He was a priest and scholar; his astrological teaching sat beside his critical astronomy. Many of his papers were lost, so evaluate his reach with caution.
