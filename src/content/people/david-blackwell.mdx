David Blackwell was an American statistician and mathematician at the University of California, Berkeley. He worked in probability, statistics, game theory and dynamic programming.

## The central question

How much can you improve a guess by using everything you know?

The theorem has two names because Rao and Blackwell found related results independently. His 1965 election to the National Academy of Sciences is widely noted as a first for a Black scholar.
