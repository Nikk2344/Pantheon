Democritus developed ancient atomism with Leucippus. His writings survive mainly through later reports and fragments.

## The central question

Can order emerge from matter in motion without a guiding purpose?

Ancient atoms were a philosophical proposal, not the experimentally established atoms of modern chemistry. Ask what observations could distinguish an explanatory model from a suggestive picture.
