Willard Van Orman Quine was a Harvard philosopher and logician. He was a leading figure in twentieth-century analytic philosophy.

## The central question

Is there any sharp line between what we know by meaning and what we know by experience?

His arguments have been criticised from many angles, and his own views changed. Reading the original article is a good way to see the disputes at first hand.
