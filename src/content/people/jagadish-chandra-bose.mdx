Jagadish Chandra Bose investigated millimeter waves, radio science, and plant physiology, developing instruments for detecting subtle responses.

## The central question

How can instruments reveal responses too small or fast for ordinary perception?

His plant work should not be turned into a claim that plants think like humans. The serious point is instrumentation, response, and continuity in physiological investigation.
