Yi Hwang, known as Toegye, was a Joseon scholar of Neo-Confucianism. He emphasised the primacy of principle (i) and moral cultivation.

## The central question

How can moral feeling be rooted in principle without being reduced to mere emotion?

The Four-Seven debate is technical and continued for generations, so no summary settles it. Read it as an argument within the Zhu Xi tradition.
