Grothendieck rebuilt algebraic geometry in the 1950s and 1960s around schemes and topoi, and turned away from the mathematical establishment in later life.

## The central question

What if the right abstraction makes hard problems almost obvious?

His work is highly abstract and demanding. It is better to learn its aims and consequences before trying to follow the formalism.
