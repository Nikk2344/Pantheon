Linnaeus was a Swedish physician and botanist who made a standard system of names and ranks for plants and animals. His works are the starting point of modern zoological and botanical naming.

## The central question

How should the diversity of living things be named and ordered?

His system was designed around fixed species, before evolution was a scientific theory. His human varieties in later editions of Systema Naturae reflect and reinforced racial prejudice of the period.
