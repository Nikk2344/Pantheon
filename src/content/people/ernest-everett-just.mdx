Ernest Everett Just was a biologist who studied fertilization and early development in marine invertebrate eggs. He worked at Howard University and at Woods Hole.

## The central question

Is a cell best understood as a bag of parts or as an organised whole?

His career was constrained by racial barriers, and many of his ideas were underappreciated at the time. How far his specific claims have been confirmed is a question for specialist histories.
