Barry Marshall is an Australian physician who with Robin Warren showed that the bacterium Helicobacter pylori is involved in gastritis and peptic ulcers. They shared the 2005 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

## The central question

Can stubborn evidence overturn a settled view of disease?

The story is often told as a lone outsider against dogma. In practice, confirmation came from clinical trials and many other researchers.
