Mill’s intensive early education was followed by criticism of narrow accounts of motivation and growing concern for individuality.

## Liberty and learning

Disagreement can reveal error and prevent true beliefs becoming unexamined formulas. Experiments in living expose possibilities a uniform society could suppress.

## The difficult boundary

The harm principle does not settle every case. Actions affect others indirectly; social pressure can coerce alongside government. Mill acknowledged Harriet Taylor Mill’s intellectual importance. The extent of their collaboration remains debated.
