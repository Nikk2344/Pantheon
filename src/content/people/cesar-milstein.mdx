César Milstein was born in Bahía Blanca, Argentina, and worked in Cambridge. With Georges Köhler he developed the hybridoma method for making monoclonal antibodies, and shared the 1984 Nobel Prize with Köhler and Niels Jerne.

## The central question

Can the immune system’s precision be harnessed on demand?

Milstein and Köhler did not patent the method, which is part of the story of its rapid spread. Credit for the field is shared with the 1984 co-laureates.
