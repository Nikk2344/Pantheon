Al-Battani was an astronomer from Harran who observed at ar-Raqqa and elsewhere in Syria. His astronomical handbook with tables was read for centuries, including in Latin translation.

## The central question

How much can astronomy improve by re-measuring what the old authorities had already measured?

Exact dates and the details of his observations are reconstructed from later copies and citations. Do not read later statements of his “discoveries” as claims that he worked outside Ptolemy’s framework.
