Beauvoir developed existentialist ethics through philosophy, fiction, and social analysis. Her work asks how material conditions enable or restrict the exercise of freedom.

## A situation is not a destiny

People act within histories and institutions they did not create. Recognizing those constraints need not deny agency.

## Read the limits

Later feminists challenge the exclusions and generalizations in her account of women. Its influence includes the arguments it made possible against itself.
