José Rizal was a Filipino physician, writer and reformer. His novels Noli Me Tangere (1887) and El Filibusterismo (1891) criticised abuses of colonial rule and shaped Philippine nationalist thought.

## The central question

How can a society reform itself under colonial rule?

He was executed by the Spanish colonial government on 30 December 1896. The extent to which he supported revolution is debated, and later memory has shaped his image.
