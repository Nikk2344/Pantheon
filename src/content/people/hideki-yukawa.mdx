Hideki Yukawa was a Japanese theoretical physicist. In 1935 he proposed that nuclear forces are carried by a new massive particle, and he received the 1949 Nobel Prize in Physics.

## The central question

Can the force that holds a nucleus together be explained by an exchanged particle?

The particle first found in cosmic rays proved not to be the one he predicted, and the pion was identified later. Read the history as a sequence of revisions, not a single confirmation.
