Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis was an Indian statistician who founded the Indian Statistical Institute in 1931. He gave his name to a distance measure and developed large-scale sample surveys.

## The central question

How can a nation measure itself well enough to plan?

His role in the model behind India’s Second Five-Year Plan was influential but the plan and its economics remain debated. Separate his statistical work from the policy judgements.
