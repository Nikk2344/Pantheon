Voltaire was a French writer, historian, and critic. His essays, plays, and satire made him a leading voice of the Enlightenment.

## The central question

How should reason respond to intolerance?

Voltaire is often credited with the line “I disapprove of what you say, but I will defend to the death your right to say it”, which was written by S. G. Tallentyre in 1906 to sum up his attitude. He also expressed views, including about Jews and Africans, that are rightly criticised.
