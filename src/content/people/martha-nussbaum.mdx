Nussbaum draws on ancient philosophy, literature, and political thought to examine flourishing and justice.

## The central question

What must people actually be able to do and to be?

A capability is an opportunity, not a requirement to use it. Ask how a society can protect meaningful options without forcing everyone into a single ideal life.
