Ibn al-Nafis was a physician trained in Damascus who later worked in Cairo. His commentary on anatomy in Avicenna’s Canon contains a description of blood passing from the heart to the lungs and back.

## The central question

What happens when a commentator finds that the standard authority does not match what the body does?

The passage was brought to wide attention only in 1924. Whether it influenced later European writers on the subject is unclear, and such transmission is better treated as unproven than assumed.
