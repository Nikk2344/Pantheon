Du Bois combined sociology, history, philosophy, and activism to analyze race, democracy, empire, and the struggle for equal citizenship.

## The central question

How does a society make people see themselves through the eyes of domination?

Double consciousness is not a private psychological quirk. It names a social condition produced by structures of power and recognition.
