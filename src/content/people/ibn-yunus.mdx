Ibn Yunus was an astronomer in Fatimid Cairo who compiled a large astronomical handbook (zij) under the patronage of the caliph al-Hakim. He used a long run of his own observations, including eclipses.

## The central question

What can a careful observer achieve with patient records across decades?

The zij survives only in part, and some details of his life are known from later biographers. Keep a distinction between his observations and the calculations that he borrowed.
