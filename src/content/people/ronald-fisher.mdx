Fisher was an English statistician and geneticist. He built much of the framework of modern statistics and helped combine Mendelian genetics with natural selection.

## The central question

How should an experiment be designed so that its results can be trusted?

He was a supporter of eugenics, and he argued against the claim that smoking causes lung cancer in the 1950s. His significance tests are often misread by later users.
