Shankara is a central interpreter of Advaita Vedānta, known for commentaries on major Sanskrit texts. Traditional biographies contain material that is difficult to establish historically.

## Levels of understanding

Everyday experience is not simply dismissed as meaningless. The issue is whether its distinctions have ultimate independence.

## Compare carefully

Advaita and Buddhist accounts can both challenge ordinary identity, but their claims about self and ultimate reality differ sharply. Similar vocabulary does not make them the same philosophy.
