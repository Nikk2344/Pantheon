Lucretius was a Roman poet whose only known work is De rerum natura. It presents Epicurean philosophy and atomic theory in Latin verse.

## The central question

Can understanding nature free us from fear?

Almost nothing certain is known about his life, and the story that he was driven mad by a love potion is a late tradition. The text was rediscovered in the fifteenth century.
