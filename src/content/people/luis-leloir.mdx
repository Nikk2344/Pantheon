Luis Leloir was born in Paris to Argentine parents and worked mainly in Buenos Aires. He received the 1970 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the discovery of sugar nucleotides and their role in making carbohydrates.

## The central question

How does the body build its stores of sugar from simple parts?

He is counted as Argentine by nationality, though born in France. The work was done with a small team on limited funding.
