Leeuwenhoek was a Delft draper who made single-lens microscopes of very high quality. His letters to the Royal Society reported tiny living organisms for the first time.

## The central question

What lives in a drop of water when no one has looked before?

Leeuwenhoek kept his lens-making methods private, and for a time his results were hard to reproduce. He did not develop a general theory of microbes.
