Bacon argued for a reorganization of inquiry grounded in systematic engagement with nature.

## The central question

How can inquiry resist the mind’s own habits of error?

A large pile of observations does not automatically yield a reliable explanation. Ask how a method decides which differences matter and what would count against a hypothesis.
