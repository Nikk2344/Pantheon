Kunihiko Kodaira was a Japanese mathematician who worked on complex manifolds and algebraic surfaces. He won a Fields Medal in 1954.

## The central question

How can the geometry of complex shapes be classified and understood?

These ideas are technical and need a background in geometry and sheaf theory. Begin with MacTutor’s biography and a survey of complex geometry before reading the papers.
