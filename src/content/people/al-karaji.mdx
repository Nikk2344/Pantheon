Al-Karaji worked in Baghdad and wrote on arithmetic and algebra. He treats polynomials and their operations as an arithmetic, not just as problems to be solved one by one.

## The central question

Can algebra be treated as its own arithmetic of unknowns, with its own rules?

Many of his results are known only through later quotations. Calling his proof method “mathematical induction” is a modern interpretation and is debated.
