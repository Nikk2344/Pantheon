Sharaf al-Din al-Tusi was a mathematician from Tus who taught in Mosul and elsewhere. He is best known for a treatise on equations that deals with cubics numerically and geometrically.

## The central question

How can one find the roots of an equation when no simple formula is available?

Historians disagree on how far his methods involve ideas related to derivatives. That reading is interpretive, and the text’s own terms should be kept in view.
