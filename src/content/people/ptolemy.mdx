Claudius Ptolemy worked in Alexandria in the 2nd century CE. His Almagest set out a mathematical model of the heavens that guided astronomy for well over a thousand years.

## The central question

How long can a model that works be mistaken for a description of reality?

Little is known of his life, and his birthplace is uncertain. Whether his tables rest on his own observations or partly on earlier work, notably that of Hipparchus, has long been debated.
