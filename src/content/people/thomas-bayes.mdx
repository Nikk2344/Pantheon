Thomas Bayes was an English Presbyterian minister and mathematician. His essay on probability was published after his death.

## The central question

How should evidence change what we believe?

Little is known about his life. Price edited and extended the essay, and Laplace later developed the general form of the result, so the modern theory is not his alone.
