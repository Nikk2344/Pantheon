Allan Cormack, born in Johannesburg, was a physicist who worked on the mathematics of reconstructing images from X-ray measurements. He shared the 1979 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Godfrey Hounsfield for computed tomography.

## The central question

How do you see inside a body without opening it?

Cormack’s theory and Hounsfield’s machine were developed independently. Reconstruction mathematics also has earlier roots, such as the work of Johann Radon.
