Einstein made familiar concepts feel like open questions again. What does it mean for two events to happen simultaneously? How do distant observers synchronise clocks?

## A problem hidden in ordinary words

In 1905, while working in Bern, he treated measurement procedures as part of physics. Relativity sought laws that remain consistent when observers move differently.

## More than a solitary genius

Einstein built on Maxwell, Lorentz, Planck, and others. Mathematical work with Marcel Grossmann later helped him formulate gravitation. The memorable thought experiments belong to a wider process of calculation, argument, and testing.
