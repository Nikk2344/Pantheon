Planck’s work on thermal radiation introduced the constant that became central to quantum theory.

## The central question

What if energy exchange cannot be treated as arbitrarily divisible?

Planck’s route to quantization was historically more complicated than a sudden invention of the entire quantum worldview. Einstein and later theorists extended the implications in different ways.
