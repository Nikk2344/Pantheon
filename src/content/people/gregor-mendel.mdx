Mendel was an Augustinian friar in Brno who bred pea plants for several years and counted the traits of their offspring. He found regular ratios and proposed a model of inheritance.

## The central question

Can inheritance be studied by counting?

His work was not widely noticed until it was rediscovered around 1900. Fisher argued in 1936 that the data looked closer to the expected ratios than chance would suggest, and the interpretation is still debated. Independent assortment does not hold for linked genes.
