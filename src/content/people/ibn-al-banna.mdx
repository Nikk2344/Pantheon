Ibn al-Banna was a mathematician and astronomer who spent most of his life in Morocco. He wrote concise works on arithmetic and astronomy that were widely used.

## The central question

How do you teach calculation so that it can be learned and used?

His birthplace is not fully certain, and several of his works are known mainly through later commentary. Claims about the originality of some results are debated.
