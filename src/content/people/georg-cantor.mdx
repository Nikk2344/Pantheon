Cantor created set theory and showed that infinite collections can differ in size. His work was controversial and is now at the core of modern mathematics.

## The central question

Are all infinities the same size?

The opposition to his work, notably Kronecker’s, was real, but accounts that explain his illness by that opposition alone are too simple.
