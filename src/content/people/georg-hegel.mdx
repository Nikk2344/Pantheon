Hegel investigated the development of thought, history, and social freedom. His difficult texts resist reduction to a universal three-step formula.

## Follow the instability

A position can fail by its own standards and lead toward a revised understanding. The point is to reconstruct that movement, not impose a ready-made template.

## Institutions under examination

Families, civil society, and the state are parts of his account of ethical life. Whether these institutions realize freedom remains a question for argument.
