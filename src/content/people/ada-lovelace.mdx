Lovelace’s mathematical education brought her into contact with Babbage’s designs. The Analytical Engine was intended to go beyond a machine dedicated to one calculation.

## Explaining an unbuilt machine

In 1843 she translated Luigi Menabrea’s account and added notes longer than the original article. Operational detail appeared alongside reflection on what the machine could represent.

## Representation is the opening

Numbers can stand for quantities, notes, or other symbols. Lovelace recognised that the engine’s scope depended on the relationships its operations could express. That insight remains valuable even when priority claims need qualification.
