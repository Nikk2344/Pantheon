Emmanuelle Charpentier is a French microbiologist who studied how bacteria defend themselves against viruses. She shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Jennifer Doudna.

## The central question

How can a bacterial defence system be turned into an editing tool?

The work built on many earlier studies of CRISPR. The question of who has patent rights has been disputed in court and should be kept separate from the scientific record.
