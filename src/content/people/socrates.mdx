Socrates left no philosophical writings. We encounter him through Plato, Xenophon, Aristophanes, and other witnesses whose portrayals differ.

## A conversation under pressure

Give a definition of courage. Does it cover patient resistance as well as battle? Testing examples can reveal that a familiar word hides an unsettled problem.

## Read the witnesses

Plato’s Socrates is also a literary character. Separating the historical person from later philosophical reconstruction remains difficult. Ask which claims the particular source supports.
