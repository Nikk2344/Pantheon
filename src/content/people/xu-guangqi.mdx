Xu Guangqi was a Ming official and scholar. He worked with the Jesuit Matteo Ricci to translate the first books of Euclid’s Elements and later led calendar reform.

## The central question

What happens when two scientific traditions meet and try to translate each other?

The Agricultural Treatise was edited and finished by others after his death. His Christian conversion and his scientific work are both documented, and scholars interpret the relationship between them differently.
