Sri Aurobindo was a Bengali-born nationalist who later became a philosopher and yogi. He wrote on an evolutionary idea of consciousness in works like The Life Divine and Essays on the Gita.

## The central question

Is human consciousness a stage on the way to something more?

His claims rest on spiritual experience as much as on argument, and they are described here as his own position and not as established fact. Assess his later politics in its own historical context.
