Sartre developed existentialism through philosophy, fiction, theater, and political engagement.

## The central question

How do we evade responsibility for the choices through which we define ourselves?

Calling someone free does not mean that all options are equally available. Compare Sartre’s account with Beauvoir’s analysis of material and social constraints.
