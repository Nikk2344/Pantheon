Luis Alvarez was an American experimental physicist who won the 1968 Nobel Prize in Physics for work on elementary particles using the hydrogen bubble chamber. He also co-authored the 1980 impact hypothesis for the end-Cretaceous extinction.

## The central question

Can particle physics shed light on an extinction long ago?

The impact hypothesis was initially disputed and later gained support from the Chicxulub crater, while the role of other factors is still discussed.
