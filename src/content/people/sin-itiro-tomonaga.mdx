Sin-Itiro Tomonaga was a Japanese physicist. He shared the 1965 Nobel Prize in Physics with Julian Schwinger and Richard Feynman for work on quantum electrodynamics.

## The central question

How can a theory that gives infinite answers be made to predict real numbers?

His early work appeared in Japanese journals and was hard to reach internationally at the time. For the fuller story of the three-way credit, read his Nobel lecture and histories of QED.
