Sanger developed methods to read the order of the units in proteins and later in DNA. He won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry twice, in 1958 and 1980.

## The central question

Can the exact sequence of a biological molecule be read?

The 1980 prize was shared with Walter Gilbert and Paul Berg, and Sanger’s method was one of two independent approaches published in 1977. The work was collaborative.
