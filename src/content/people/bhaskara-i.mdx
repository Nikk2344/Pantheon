Bhaskara I was a 7th-century Indian mathematician and astronomer who wrote the earliest known commentary on Aryabhata’s work. He is also credited with the Mahabhaskariya and the Laghubhaskariya.

## The central question

How do commentators carry a difficult text to wider readers?

He should not be confused with Bhaskara II, who lived about five centuries later. His dates and place of work are inferred from his texts and from the later tradition.
