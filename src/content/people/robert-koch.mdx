Koch was a German physician who developed methods for isolating bacteria in pure culture. He identified the causes of anthrax, tuberculosis, and cholera.

## The central question

What would count as proof that a particular microbe causes a particular disease?

The postulates do not fit all infections, for example those with healthy carriers or microbes that cannot be grown in culture. His claim in 1890 of a tuberculosis treatment, tuberculin, did not work as a cure.
