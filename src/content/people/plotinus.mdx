Plotinus developed a powerful interpretation of Plato. Porphyry later organized his treatises into the Enneads.

## The central question

How can multiplicity depend on a unity beyond ordinary description?

Procession is not a physical event in which pieces detach from a larger object. Ask how a metaphor of flowing can illuminate dependence and where it might mislead.
