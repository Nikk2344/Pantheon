Li Shizhen was a Ming physician. He spent decades compiling the Bencao Gangmu, a classified survey of medicinal substances, published after his death.

## The central question

How do you organise knowledge of thousands of substances so that it can be checked and used?

The work includes older material, and some of its remedies and explanations reflect the medicine of its time. It is a catalogue of what was believed and recorded, not a modern pharmacology.
