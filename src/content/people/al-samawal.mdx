Al-Samawal was born in Baghdad to a Jewish family and later wrote that he converted to Islam. He wrote a major algebra, al-Bahir, which extends arithmetic to polynomials.

## The central question

How far can the rules of arithmetic be extended to cover new kinds of quantity?

Our information about his life is largely drawn from his own writings. Some claims about his use of negative or zero powers should be read as interpretations of his notation.
