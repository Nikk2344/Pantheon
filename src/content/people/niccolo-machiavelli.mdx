Machiavelli analyzed republican liberty, princely power, military organization, and political necessity with unusual attention to conflict and historical examples.

## The central question

How should political action be judged when power, danger, and public order collide?

Machiavelli is not merely a handbook for cruelty. Compare The Prince with the Discourses before deciding what political realism means in his work.
