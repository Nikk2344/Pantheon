Wegener was a German meteorologist and polar explorer. In 1912 he proposed that the continents were once joined and have drifted apart.

## The central question

Could the continents have moved?

Most geologists rejected the idea in his lifetime because he had no convincing mechanism. It was revived in the 1950s and 1960s with evidence from the sea floor and palaeomagnetism, in the form of plate tectonics. He died on an expedition to Greenland.
