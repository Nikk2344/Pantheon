Plato rarely speaks in a straightforward authorial voice. Characters question, answer, and encounter difficulties; the dialogue form is part of the work.

## Questions beyond slogans

The dialogues ask how opinion differs from knowledge and what makes a life good. Dramatic settings and unresolved passages make extracting a single final doctrine hazardous.

## The cost of knowing better

The cave is also political. Someone who sees differently may struggle to explain the change. Whether that difference gives authority over others is a further question, not an automatic consequence of the image.
