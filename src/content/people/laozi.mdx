Laozi is the traditional figure associated with the Daodejing. The text’s formation and the existence of a single historical author remain disputed.

## Unforced does not mean absent

Wuwei is often translated as nonaction. It can point to action without forcing a rigid scheme onto circumstances, rather than simply doing nothing.

## Read more than one translation

Terms such as dao and de do not map neatly onto one English word. Compare how a passage changes when its key terms are translated differently.
