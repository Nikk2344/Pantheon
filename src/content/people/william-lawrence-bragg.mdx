William Lawrence Bragg was born in Adelaide and worked in Britain. He shared the 1915 Nobel Prize in Physics with his father William Henry Bragg for using X-rays to study crystal structure, and remains the youngest physics laureate.

## The central question

Can the pattern of scattered X-rays reveal where atoms sit?

His father’s role is important: the work was joint and the experimental spectrometer was largely developed by W. H. Bragg.
