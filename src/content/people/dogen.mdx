Dogen was a Japanese Buddhist monk who founded the Soto Zen school in Japan. His writings, especially the Shobogenzo, explore practice, time and being.

## The central question

What if practice is not a means to enlightenment but its expression?

The text is demanding and translations vary. Comparisons with Heidegger or others are interpretive, and Dogen himself writes from within Buddhist doctrine.
