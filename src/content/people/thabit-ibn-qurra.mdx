Thabit ibn Qurra was born in Harran and worked in Baghdad, where he translated and revised Greek mathematical texts and wrote original works. His interests included number theory, geometry and astronomy.

## The central question

What can translation teach a mathematician about how to go beyond the texts translated?

His astronomical theory of “trepidation” is sometimes cited as a precursor of later ideas about precession, but it is not accepted as a modern account. Sorting his own work from his revisions of translations takes care.
