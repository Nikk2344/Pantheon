Omar Khayyam was a mathematician and astronomer in the Seljuk world. He classified cubic equations and solved them with intersecting conic sections.

## The central question

Can geometry solve equations that arithmetic alone cannot?

The Rubaiyat poetry is widely attributed to him, but how many of its quatrains are really his is disputed. The mathematical work is much better documented.
