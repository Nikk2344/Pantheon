Pascal made early contributions to geometry, probability, and the study of fluids, and he built a mechanical calculator. He also wrote influential religious and philosophical works.

## The central question

How should we reason when the outcome is uncertain?

Pascal did not discover the triangle. It appears earlier in Indian, Persian, and Chinese sources, and he is credited for how he used it.
