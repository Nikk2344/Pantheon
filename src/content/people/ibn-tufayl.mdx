Ibn Tufayl was a physician and philosopher born near Granada who served at the Almohad court. He is best known for the philosophical tale Hayy ibn Yaqzan.

## The central question

Could a person raised alone, without teachers, reach truth through reason?

The work is a fictional allegory, not a treatise, so its claims should be read as arguments in narrative form. Details of his biography are drawn from later sources, and some are uncertain.
