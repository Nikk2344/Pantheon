Frank Macfarlane Burnet was an Australian virologist and immunologist. He shared the 1960 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Peter Medawar for the discovery of acquired immunological tolerance.

## The central question

How does the body learn what is itself?

The Nobel was given for tolerance, not for clonal selection; the credit for tolerance is shared with Medawar and his colleagues.
