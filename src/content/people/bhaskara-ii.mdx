Bhaskara II, also called Bhaskaracharya, wrote the Siddhanta Siromani, with its Lilavati on arithmetic and Bijaganita on algebra.

## The central question

How far can systematic algebra and astronomy be pushed with number alone?

Some descriptions say he anticipated calculus. His astronomical rates of change are a real and interesting idea, but they are not the same as a general theory of derivatives.
