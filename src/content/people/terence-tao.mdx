Terence Tao was born in Adelaide and is a mathematician at UCLA. He received the Fields Medal in 2006 for work in partial differential equations, combinatorics, harmonic analysis and additive number theory.

## The central question

How much structure is forced to appear inside apparent randomness?

He is a living mathematician, so assessments are still developing. The IMU page records the Fields citation and not a full biography.
