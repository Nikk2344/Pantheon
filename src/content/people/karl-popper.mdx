Popper developed critical rationalism and an influential account of scientific testing.

## The central question

What could show that a scientific claim is wrong?

A failed prediction may implicate auxiliary assumptions or measurement procedures. Falsifiability is not a mechanical rule that settles every scientific controversy with one observation.
