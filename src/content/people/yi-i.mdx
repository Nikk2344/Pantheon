Yi I, known as Yulgok, was a Joseon scholar and official. He developed a Neo-Confucian view in which principle and material force are closely joined, and he wrote on reform.

## The central question

How should moral philosophy be turned into practical reform of a state?

Later schools claimed both Yi Hwang and Yi I, and tradition has sometimes simplified their differences. Compare their own writings for precision.
