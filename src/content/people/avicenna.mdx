Avicenna developed an influential philosophical system while also writing extensively on medicine. His work transformed later debates in Arabic and Latin traditions.

## Necessary and contingent

A contingent thing could fail to exist. Avicenna’s metaphysics investigates how such things relate to a necessary existent.

## Test the thought experiment

Does imagining awareness without bodily sensation establish that the self is immaterial? Distinguish what the scenario asks us to conceive from what it can prove.
