Calyampudi Radhakrishna Rao was an Indian-American statistician with long careers in India and the United States. His results on estimation are standard parts of statistical theory.

## The central question

How accurately can anything be estimated from data?

The bound bears Harald Cramér’s name too, since he reached similar results independently, and the Rao–Blackwell theorem is shared with David Blackwell. Credit for such results is often shared in this field.
