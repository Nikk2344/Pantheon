Frege was a German mathematician who created modern quantificational logic and tried to base arithmetic on logic. His ideas shaped analytic philosophy.

## The central question

Can arithmetic be derived from logic alone?

In 1902 Russell wrote to Frege showing that his system in the Basic Laws of Arithmetic was inconsistent. Frege’s late diaries contain views that are widely condemned, and they should be read as part of a full account.
