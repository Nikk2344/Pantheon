Zhang Heng was a Han-dynasty astronomer, official and writer. He is credited with a water-driven armillary sphere and with a device for detecting distant earthquakes.

## The central question

How far can instruments extend what observers can know about the sky and the earth?

The seismoscope survives only in later textual description, and modern reconstructions differ on how it worked. Treat claims about its exact mechanism as reconstruction.
