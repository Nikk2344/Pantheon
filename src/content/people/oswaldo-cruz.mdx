Oswaldo Cruz was a Brazilian physician and bacteriologist who led sanitation campaigns in Rio de Janeiro in the early 1900s. The Oswaldo Cruz Foundation (Fiocruz) is named for him.

## The central question

How far can public health campaigns go in changing a city?

The 1904 compulsory smallpox vaccination provoked a major public revolt in Rio. The institutional page is a Fiocruz account and is celebratory.
