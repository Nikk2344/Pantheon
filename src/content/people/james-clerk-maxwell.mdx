Maxwell worked across colour vision, gases, and electromagnetism. A common thread was the search for mathematics that made apparently separate observations fit together.

## Making a field consistent

Faraday’s experiments suggested that space around electrical and magnetic systems had physical structure. Maxwell developed a theory connecting changing fields. The displacement-current term made room for electromagnetic waves.

## A prediction beyond the starting problem

The theory connected electrical constants to a speed already known from optics. The familiar four-equation notation is a later reformulation of Maxwell’s work, not its original presentation.
