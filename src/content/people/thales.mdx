Thales stands near the beginning of Greek natural philosophy in later accounts. Very little direct evidence survives, so his role is best treated as a landmark in a changing style of explanation.

## The central question

Can nature be explained through regular principles rather than only inherited myth?

The evidence is fragmentary and filtered through later writers. Treat Thales as a starting point for a tradition of inquiry, not as the secure author of every theorem or anecdote attached to his name.
