Fermat was a lawyer and magistrate who pursued mathematics alongside his career. His letters and marginal notes shaped number theory, probability, and early calculus.

## The central question

How much can be found by looking at whole numbers carefully?

It is very unlikely that Fermat had a proof of the general theorem. The 1995 proof uses tools that did not exist in the seventeenth century.
