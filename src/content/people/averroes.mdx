Averroes was a philosopher, physician, and jurist from Córdoba. His reception cannot be reduced to a story about Europe rediscovering Greek texts.

## One truth, different methods

He did not simply endorse two contradictory truths, one religious and one philosophical. The relation between demonstration and scriptural interpretation is more demanding.

## Intellectual travel

Translations and commentaries generated new debates rather than carrying ideas unchanged. Compare an author’s position with claims made by later followers and critics.
