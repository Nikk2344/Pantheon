Vasubandhu was a Buddhist scholar of Gandhara in what is now Pakistan. His works on Abhidharma and on Yogacara, the school of consciousness-only, were widely studied across Asia.

## The central question

Is the world as we experience it made of mind alone?

Scholars debate whether one or two authors lie behind the works traditionally ascribed to him, and the dating is uncertain. How the idealist reading should be understood is also disputed.
