Nilakantha Somayaji was a scholar of the Kerala tradition. His Tantrasamgraha of 1501 revised the standard model of the inner planets, and he also wrote a commentary on Aryabhata.

## The central question

Can a model be revised when observation disagrees with a classical text?

Modern readers sometimes describe his planetary model as near to Tycho Brahe’s. That is a structural comparison only, and it does not imply influence.
