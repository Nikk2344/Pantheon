Al-Kashi was a mathematician and astronomer from Kashan who worked at Samarkand under Ulugh Beg. He is known for very accurate numerical computations.

## The central question

How accurate can a computation be made, and how can one show it?

Decimal fractions appear earlier in the Islamic world and elsewhere, so “inventor” is the wrong word. He brought them to a high standard of use.
