Robin Wall Kimmerer is a botanist and a citizen of the Potawatomi Nation. She teaches at the State University of New York College of Environmental Science and Forestry and wrote Braiding Sweetgrass.

## The central question

Can Indigenous knowledge and laboratory science speak to each other?

She is a living scholar, and her book is part memoir and essay. It should not be read as speaking for all Indigenous nations.
