Wallace was a naturalist who collected specimens in the Amazon and the Malay Archipelago. He independently reached the idea of evolution by natural selection.

## The central question

What can the distribution of living things tell us about how they came to be?

Wallace’s role is sometimes minimised, and sometimes overstated against Darwin. His interests in spiritualism and some other views were controversial in his own time.
