Fibonacci, or Leonardo of Pisa, wrote Liber Abaci in 1202. It taught European merchants and scholars how to calculate with the Hindu–Arabic numerals.

## The central question

What changes when a society adopts a better way of writing numbers?

The sequence was described in India centuries before him, and the name “Fibonacci” was popularised later. Claims that it governs most natural forms are usually exaggerated.
