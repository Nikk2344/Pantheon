Bentham connected utilitarian moral reasoning with ambitious proposals for legal and political reform.

## The central question

How should institutions account for everyone’s pleasures and pains?

A calculation requires choices about what counts, whose interests matter, and how comparisons work. Those questions are substantive ethical commitments, not just arithmetic.
