Nietzsche wrote in aphorisms, essays, and deliberately unsettling literary forms. His thought is not a simple instruction to pursue power over others.

## An origin is not yet a refutation

Showing where a belief came from does not by itself show it false. Ask how a genealogy also assesses the value’s effects and justifications.

## Read with context

Isolated lines are easy to appropriate. Track the work, speaker, and target of a passage before turning it into a motto.
