Shinya Yamanaka is a Japanese stem-cell researcher. With colleagues he showed that adult cells can be reprogrammed to a pluripotent state, work that earned the 2012 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

## The central question

Can a specialised cell be made to remember how to become anything?

The therapeutic promise remains under study, and safety and reproducibility are active issues. The Nobel was shared with John Gurdon, whose earlier work on nuclear reprogramming prepared the ground.
