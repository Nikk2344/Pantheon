Weil joined philosophical reflection with political activism, factory work, and religious inquiry.

## The central question

What kind of attention does another person’s suffering demand?

Her demanding ideals invite questions about self-erasure and political effectiveness. Consider whether attending to suffering also requires changes in the institutions producing it.
