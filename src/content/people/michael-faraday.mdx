Faraday began as a bookbinder’s apprentice. Reading and attending lectures helped him enter the Royal Institution, where he developed extraordinary experimental skill.

## The signal that disappears

A brief needle movement was more revealing than a steady reading. Making or breaking a current changed magnetic conditions and produced a transient effect in another circuit.

## Thinking in fields

Faraday described physical influence through lines of force in space. Maxwell later gave related ideas a mathematical formulation. Their connection shows how an experimental picture can guide a theory without already containing its final equations.
