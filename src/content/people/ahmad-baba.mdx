Ahmad Baba was a scholar of Timbuktu trained in Islamic law and related sciences. After the Moroccan conquest of Songhai he was taken to Marrakesh, where he continued to teach and write.

## The central question

What is a scholar’s duty when his city falls?

Figures for the size of his library are only traditional estimates, and the details of his treatise and its reception should be read in specialist studies. Describe what he argued, not what later traditions wished he argued.
